# A8 Horizon dependence of the freed fraction and of the fraction match

Script: `experiments/revision/A8_horizon.py` -> `data/derived/rev_A8.json`; registry `notes/A8.yaml` (prefix `RevAeight`). Existing data only, CPU a few seconds.

## What was done

- The outcome at horizon T comes from the recorded exit time:
  - snapshots: the blocker's exit time ≤ T;
  - pairs: `freed` and first member exit `first_t` ≤ T (the `make_table3.py derive` rule);
  - whole runs: `complete` and `evacuation_time` ≤ T.
- Horizons: 60 s and 120 s for snapshots and pairs (the quench window is 120 s); 300 s and 600 s for whole runs (t_max = 600 s).
- Δ = mean over units of (surrogate freed fraction of the unit − ORCA outcome of the unit). The 95% percentile bootstrap uses 2000 resamples and a fresh PCG64(12345) per comparison. Whole runs also get the Newcombe paired interval and an exact McNemar test.
- "Fraction-matched at T" means the CI of Δ covers 0. "Horizon-specific" means matched at one horizon and not at the other.
- Scans: Δ(T) of every noise arm against ORCA (RVO2) for T = 10–120 s in 5 s steps (snapshots) and 150–600 s in 25 s steps (whole runs).
- Check: the 120 s and 600 s values reproduce Table 3: lone keystones −0.01 [−0.10, 0.08]; whole runs −0.01 [−0.10, 0.08]; pair legacy +0.02 [−0.15, 0.22].

## Verdict

- Matching a deterministic resolver with a stochastic surrogate is horizon-specific for every noise surrogate.
  - Force-coupled ORCA and its deterministic parts reach their final outcome within 14 s (lone traps) or 161 s (whole runs). After that their freed fraction is flat.
  - The noise freed fraction keeps rising with T. The surrogate is therefore fraction-matched only within a window of T: lone keystone σ = 0.07 from 70 s, whole runs σ* from 425 s, second trap σ = 0.07 against RVO2 up to 50 s only.
- The pair bias is deterministic and frees by 23 s, so its match does not depend on the horizon in 60–120 s.

**Claim-relevant sentences**

- "Per-step noise at σ=0.07 m/s, five seeds per snapshot, matches the rate of ORCA (RVO2)". This holds at 120 s and fails at 60 s (Δ = −0.09 [−0.16, −0.01]). The sentence should name the horizon.
- Sect. 6.2, "so σ* matches completion within this horizon only". Confirmed: at 300 s, Δ = −0.15 [−0.23, −0.06].
