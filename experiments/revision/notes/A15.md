## Flags

1. **Two exit events.** Quench outcomes (freed, time to free; Sects. 5.3, 6.1, 6.3, 6.4, Figs. 6-7, Table 3) count the simulator's exit, |x − g| < 0.5 m with g = (11, 5) m (Table 1), i.e. about 0.5 m beyond the door plane. Escape times and rates (E3, E4, N3; Sects. 5.2, 6.1 time-step signature, Figs. 4-5) count the blocker's centre crossing the door plane x ≥ 10 m. The paper states neither the escape event nor the difference. The same snapshot freed in a quench is thus timed later than in an escape run.
2. **E3 censoring is not stated.** Sect. 6.1 (Time-Step Signature) gives no censoring time; the code censors at 300 s, at 900 s for per-step noise at Δt = 0.02 s, and the ORCA (RVO2) runs at 300 s. "ORCA (RVO2) ... frees the same snapshots at every Δt" holds within 300 s.
3. **The 120 s window binds for force-coupled ORCA at the pair.** Every held ORCA pair run, 39 (RVO2) and 25 (legacy), still moves at 120 s; none came to rest. ORCA's pair rates and the "> 120" time to free (Table 3) are freed fractions at T = 120 s of runs that have not stopped. At the lone trap 9 of 13 held ORCA (RVO2) runs still move at 120 s. All freeings occur before 51 s.
4. **Different stillness thresholds by design.** Whole runs and snapshots use |v| < 0.01 m/s for 10 s (Table 1); quench stops use 1e-3 m/s for 10 s (Sect. 3.4); velocity-coupled quenches use 1e-3 too. Paper and code agree on both; the factor-10 difference is not explained in the text. "Still move at 600 s" (Sect. 6.2) and "come to rest" mean "the 0.01 m/s detector did not / did fire"; the text does not say so.
5. **Unstated horizons and rules** behind stated numbers: keystone test 120 s with the 1e-3 m/s stop (Sect. 5.1; also the failure classes of Sects. 6.2 and 6.4); second-trap force trace 20 s (Sect. 6.1 "In held runs"); static pair prediction 120 s, freed at x > 10.3 m, held at max|F| < 0.05 N (Sect. 5.3); arch window 1.5 m around (10, 5) with x < 10.3 m (Sect. 5.4); stall definition excludes the first interval (Sects. 4.1, 6.2); velocity-variance window (2 s burn-in, x < 9.95 m, 60 s runs at β = 6, cells with ≥ 200 samples; Sect. 4.4); barrier end points x < 9.95 m and x = 10.35 m (Sect. 4.5); abreast = opposite sides with blocker > 0.3 m from the axis (Sect. 6.1); A-4 jump threshold 1e-3 m/s (Sect. 6.1); door-zone arrival x > 9.4 m, |y − 5| < 0.6 m (Sect. 5.1 arrival deciles); Savitzky-Golay window 1.48 s (Sect. 7); the 600 s cap of the E1 library, E5 and E7 (Sects. 5.1, 5.2).
6. **One horizon differs from all others:** the N6 near-fold quench runs 300 s without a stationary stop; its macros (`RunNSixQuench*`) are not used in the text, so no stated number depends on it.
7. **Paper value = code value** for every stated quantity: 0.01 m/s / 10 s, 1e-3 m/s / 10 s, 120 s, 600 s, 300/900 s (E4), 10 s stall, 10 s arch persistence and 0.1 s checks, 1e-4 m/s TTC cutoff (coded as |Δv|² ≥ 1e-8), τ_max = 8 s, τ_h = 5 s, R = 3 m, 0.1 J floor, 1e-9 m replays, CroMa 0.3-2.5 m, 2 m, 0.3 m/s, 0.74 m, 2 s. No mismatch found.
