# A5 Snapshot selection: rules, counts and the balance of the 26 second traps

Scripts: `experiments/revision/A5_selection.py` (analysis only; no simulation) -> `data/derived/rev_A5.json`;
`experiments/revision/A5_extra_second_traps.py` (job builder for the 134 second traps E2 did not draw; not launched).
Registry: `experiments/revision/notes/A5.yaml` (44 macros, prefix `RevAfive`, validated with tools/make_numbers.py).
The rules below were checked by re-deriving every selection from the inputs and asserting equality with
`data/derived/selection.json` and `data/E7b/e7b_barrier.jsonl`.

## 1. Lone-trap library: from whole runs to the 60 E2 snapshots

| Step | Rule (source) | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Whole runs | E1b (corrected-forward), room bottleneck, w = 0.8 m, run seeds 42-241 (`experiments/E1/e1_library.py`) | 200 |
| Complete | all agents exit within 600 s | 4 |
| Deadlocks | deadlock criterion of Table 1 holds without an exit; state saved (`data/E1/library/E1b_seed<N>.pkl`) | 196 (196 pickles) |
| Keystone test | restart under SFM (C1), sigma = 0, 120 s, stop when stationary (`experiments/E1/keystone.py`): K1 = blocker alone (blocker = residual agent nearest (10, 5)); K2 = blocker removed | 196 classified |
| lone keystone | K1 held, every other agent exits under K2 | 34 |

| group standoff | K1 freed (blocker not held alone; negative margin, pair at the jamb faces; seeds 45, 101) | 2 |
| E2 | all 34 lone keystones (seed ascending) + 26 drawn by `numpy.random.Generator(PCG64(2026)).choice(160 second-trap seeds ascending, 26, replace=False)`, then sorted (`experiments/selection.py e2`, plan C15) | 60 |

The second-trap pool is the whole population: the draw was made from all 160 second traps of the

(0 of 160), so nothing was excluded before the draw. Re-running the draw reproduces the 26 seeds exactly
(46, 55, 60, 73, 74, 75, 103, 104, 111, 112, 113, 130, 158, 162, 170, 172, 174, 178, 186, 187, 192, 202, 209, 223, 230, 237).

## 2. "Of 36 that qualify" (E3) and "of 32 that qualify" (E4)

Both start from the 60 E2 snapshots and the string-method barriers (`experiments/BAR/bar.py`), filtered by the
C15a validity gate (`experiments/BAR/gate.py`): 4 of 60 fail, and all 4 are second traps
(seed 75: barrier changes by 100% from 62 to 124 images; 172: climbing image not converged;
174: string not converged at 124 images; 209: barrier changes by 5.7%). 56 accepted (34 lone keystones, 22 second traps).

- **E3**: gate-accepted and collective barrier Delta U >= 3 J (full condition, every residual agent mobile).
  36 qualify (26 lone keystones, 10 second traps); 20 of the 56 lie below 3 J. The 20 used are the ranks
  round(linspace(0, 35, 20)) of the qualifying list sorted by (Delta U, seed): 15 lone keystones and 5 second
  traps, Delta U = 3.9-57 J.
- **E4**: gate-accepted and n_resid >= 2, where n_resid counts the blocker (blocker plus at least one
  neighbour); no barrier threshold. 32 qualify (10 lone keystones, 22 second traps); 24 accepted snapshots are a
  single agent. The 10 used are ranks round(linspace(0, 31, 10)) sorted by (Delta U, seed): 3 lone keystones, 7
  second traps, Delta U = 0.021-37 J (this is why the 0.021 J snapshot appears in E4 and is skipped in the full
  condition). 15 snapshots satisfy both rules; 2 are in both E3 and E4.

Note for the text: the paper states neither the 3 J threshold of E3 nor that "qualify" includes gate acceptance;
the counts 36 and 32 are correct for these rules.

## 3. Pair barriers (PairBarN = 12)

The pair library is 46 snapshots (E7b, B_w = 0.04 m, run seeds 242-561). `experiments/E7b/e7b_barrier.py jobs()`
sorts them by mu1 (smallest Hessian eigenvalue, key `lambda1`, from `e7b_static.jsonl`) and takes ranks
round(linspace(0, 45, 12)) = 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 25, 29, 33, 37, 41, 45, so the selection covers the full mu1
range 65-121 N/m. Each snapshot has two exit channels (one per member, `pairlib.PairBarField`) through the same
gate; 21 of 24 channels pass, every snapshot has at least one accepted channel, and the pair barrier is the
lower accepted channel. All 12 enter (PairBarN = 12).

Draft sentence: "The pair barriers were computed on 12 of the 46 pair snapshots at evenly spaced ranks of
mu1, from the softest to the stiffest."

## 4. Are the 26 second traps representative of the 160?

By construction the draw is uniform without replacement from the full population, so it is unbiased by design.
As a check, the 26 drawn were compared with the 134 not drawn on 13 covariates fixed before any quench
(two-sided Mann-Whitney and KS; Fisher for the binary one; Holm over the 13 Mann-Whitney / Fisher p).

| Covariate | median drawn | median not drawn | SMD | p (MW / Fisher) | p (KS) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| blocker margin_C1 (N) | 125 | 122 | 0.02 | 0.730 | 0.957 |
| n_resid | 3 | 3 | -0.01 | 0.704 | 0.997 |
| blocker r (m) | 0.300 | 0.293 | 0.20 | 0.323 | 0.339 |
| blocker v0 (m/s) | 1.06 | 1.00 | 0.18 | 0.317 | 0.558 |
| blocker tau (s) | 0.524 | 0.543 | -0.16 | 0.812 | 0.382 |
| blocker lateral offset (m) | 0.023 | 0.017 | 0.45 | 0.147 | 0.210 |
| blocker distance to door centre (m) | 0.320 | 0.312 | 0.43 | 0.138 | 0.078 |
| deadlock time (s) | 153 | 155 | -0.23 | 0.429 | 0.143 |
| agents held without the blocker (K2) | 1 | 2 | -0.30 | 0.142 | 0.579 |
| second trap's margin_C1 (N) | 87 | 73 | 0.04 | 0.571 | 0.521 |
| second trap's r (m) | 0.282 | 0.278 | 0.20 | 0.441 | 0.280 |
| run seed | 160 | 146 | -0.01 | 0.991 | 0.927 |
| second trap stuck alone (K1b held) | 26/26 | 125/134 | 0.38 | 0.357 (Fisher) | - |

Smallest raw p = 0.138 (blocker distance to door), Holm-adjusted p = 1.000 for every covariate; largest |SMD| = 0.45.
Omnibus: the largest |SMD| over the 13 covariates is exceeded or matched by 30.5% of 20000 random 26-of-160
draws (p = 0.305). No covariate differs. One feature worth stating: none of the 9 second traps that are freed
when alone was drawn (expected 1.5 under the draw; hypergeometric probability of none 0.19; two-sided
Fisher p = 0.357), so the E2 second-trap stratum contains only stacks in which the second agent
is itself stuck alone.

Draft sentences: "The 26 second traps were drawn uniformly without replacement from all 160 (numpy PCG64, seed
2026); they do not differ from the 134 not drawn in blocker margin, residual count, blocker radius, v0, tau,
position or the second agent's margin (smallest of 13 Holm-adjusted p = 1.00). All 26 have a second agent that
is itself stuck alone, as do 125 of the 134 not drawn."

## 5. Screening the remaining 134 second traps (run; results in A5x.md)

The sample is fair, so the extra screening is a precision question (n = 26 at the second trap; the RVO2 vs
drive-part contrast rests on 7:0 discordant pairs). `experiments/revision/A5_extra_second_traps.py` ran on the 134
not drawn, 21 jobs per snapshot (2814 jobs): noise sigma = 0.07 m/s x 5 seeds (seeds 90000 + 100 k + rep, k = index
in the 160 sorted by seed; disjoint from E2's 7000-12904 and every other range in experiments/SEEDS.md),
force-coupled ORCA (RVO2) and ORCA (legacy) unperturbed + 5 replays each (perturb seeds 900-904), drive part,
push +, constraint part (RVO2) and the SFM restart (C1). E2 arm definitions, job_id format and the `quench_e2`
outcome are imported from `experiments/E2/e2_quench.py`; output `data/revision/A5/a5_quench.jsonl`. CPU time 3.3
h (0.83 h wall on 4 workers). Analysis: `experiments/revision/A5x_analyze.py`, notes/A5x.md.

## Claim check

No sentence of the paper is contradicted. The paper's "Of 60 snapshots, 34 are lone keystones and 26 second
traps" (Sect. 6) does not say the 26 are a random draw from 160; the E3 / E4 "that qualify" counts omit the rules
(gate acceptance; Delta U >= 3 J for E3; n_resid >= 2 counting the blocker for E4). These are omissions in the
text, not errors in the numbers.

CPU time: smoke test 243 s of simulation on one worker; analysis about 10 s.
