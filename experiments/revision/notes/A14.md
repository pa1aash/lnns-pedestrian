## 3. The defect in three lines

With n̂ the outward normal of the leg (RVO2), the legacy normal is
  n_legacy = u/|u| = -sgn(v_rel · n̂) n̂,  u = (v_rel·d̂) d̂ - v_rel.
It equals n̂ only while v_rel lies inside the velocity obstacle; for v_rel outside (no predicted contact) the half-plane is inverted, the current velocity always violates it, and it demands a velocity change of |u|/2 towards the leg. When v_rel crosses the leg, |u| passes through 0 and n flips by 180°, so the permitted half-plane jumps to its complement at a continuous state: the discontinuity behind the class-N replay flips (A-4: leg-normal flip in 4 of the 5 first discontinuities).

Static check on 200 000 random non-colliding pairs (|x| in (R, 3] m, |v| up to 2.7 m/s, τ_h = 5 s):
- branch: 92 261 cap, 107 739 leg; the cap half-planes coincide (normals within 2.6e-8 rad, points within 7e-16 m/s);
- leg, v_rel outside the VO (direct geometry: no t ≤ τ_h with |x - v t| < R): 83 856, of which 83 856 (1.00) have n_legacy = -n_RVO2;
- leg, v_rel inside the VO: 23 883, all with n_legacy = n_RVO2;
- the current velocity v_i violates the legacy leg half-plane in 107 739 of 107 739 leg pairs (1.00); RVO2's in 0 of 83 856 outside and 23 883 of 23 883 inside;
- left/right choice: legacy det(x, v_rel) and RVO2 det(x, w), w = v_rel - x/τ_h, agree in all leg pairs; they are identical algebraically since det(x, x) = 0.
At exact rest (v_rel = 0) w = -x/τ_h and the cap branch always applies, so the two solvers build the same half-planes at a frozen snapshot; in a creep sample (|v| ≤ 0.02 m/s) only 283 of 200 000 pairs are in the leg branch. The defect acts once agents creep.

Other differences of the legacy solver from RVO2 (not the defect): infeasible constraints are skipped (`orca.py:178-200`, `continue`) instead of LP3; neighbours in KD-tree order, not nearest first. A-4 found 0 of 16 discontinuity events that drop a constraint.

## 5. Claim check

- Paper, Sect. 3.2 and Table 1: "the legacy linear-program solver, which has a leg-normal sign defect". The defect is in the half-plane construction that feeds the LP (`_halfplane_normal`), not in the LP itself. Not a change of claim; a wording precision.
- Code comment `code/sim/steering/orca_rvo2.py:12-13` lists "left/right leg chosen by det(relPos, w) ... not det(relPos, v_rel)" as a difference. The two tests are identical (det(x, x) = 0; 107 739 of 107 739 agree). The comment is wrong; no result depends on it (code/ is not edited here).

## 6. Draft sentences

"The legacy solver is the ORCA implementation of the conference code [11] (repository pedestrian-abs, `sim/steering/orca.py`, commit 9762177). For a leg of the velocity obstacle it takes the half-plane normal as $\mathbf u/|\mathbf u|$, where $\mathbf u$ points from the relative velocity to its projection on the leg; this equals $-\mathrm{sgn}(\Delta\mathbf v\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})\,\hat{\mathbf n}$ with $\hat{\mathbf n}$ the outward leg normal, so the half-plane is inverted whenever the relative velocity lies outside the velocity obstacle and switches to its complement when the relative velocity crosses the leg."

"At rest every pair falls in the cut-off branch, where both solvers build the same half-plane; the defect acts only once agents move. All C3 and C4 results of [11] used this solver."
