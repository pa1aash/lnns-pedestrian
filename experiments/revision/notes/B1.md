# B1 Converse of the stuck-alone criterion

**Done.** experiments/revision/B1_converse.py. Population: the 20 000 spawned agents of the 200 E1b runs (15 314 with negative
margin, 4686 positive; margin g_max(r) − m v0/τ, default walls, w = 0.8 m). 200 per sign, spread over the margin (sorted by
margin, ranks round(linspace(0, n−1, 200))). Each alone, at rest on the door axis: positive margin at its rest state (first point
upstream where the backward wall force equals the drive); negative margin (no rest state) at the axial position of g_max
(9.83 m, 0.17 m before the door plane) and 1.0 m upstream of it. C1, CF, Δt = 0.01 s, 60 s; pass = centre reaches the door plane.

**Result.** Negative margin: 200/200 pass from the g_max position (Wilson lower bound 0.98; median 0.67 s, longest 46 s at
margin −0.007 N) and 200/200 from 1 m upstream (median 1.4 s, at most 2.5 s). Positive margin: 0/200 pass (upper bound 0.019),
margins 0.008–454 N. The criterion is exact in both directions to within 0.01 N.

**Draft sentence.** Released alone at rest on the door axis, all 400 runs of 200 sampled agents with negative margin pass the
door within 60 s (down to a margin of −0.007 N, from the position of the largest wall force), and none of 200 with positive
margin leaves its rest state, so Eq. (stuck) is necessary and sufficient.
