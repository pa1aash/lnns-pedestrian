# A7: paired difference tests in place of the interval-overlap rule

Script: `experiments/revision/A7_paired.py` (existing data only, no simulation; about 7 s CPU).
Output: `data/derived/rev_A7.json`; registry `experiments/revision/notes/A7.yaml` (241 macros, prefix `RevAseven`, validated with `tools/make_numbers.py`); LaTeX table snippet `experiments/revision/notes/A7_table.tex` (booktabs, not `\input` anywhere).

## Referee objection

Protocol step 5 (Sect. 4.2) reads: "A probe separates two perturbations when their 95% intervals for that signature exclude each other, or when one shows the predicted effect and the other its absence; replay and handedness are exact counts." Two intervals that exclude each other give a conservative and uncalibrated test of a difference. The two intervals also ignore the pairing: both perturbations run on the same snapshots or spawn seeds. The replacement below tests the paired difference directly and corrects for multiple probes.

## What was done (exact definitions)

Each comparison is a surrogate (or the reference push) against force-coupled ORCA on the same units. Every effect is surrogate minus ORCA. Units: for a lone-trap or pair snapshot, a deterministic arm contributes its unperturbed run and a noise arm contributes all its seeds (the snapshot is the resampling unit); for whole runs, the unit is the spawn seed.

Comparisons: (1a) noise σ = 0.07 m/s, five seeds (E2), against ORCA (RVO2) on 34 lone keystones; (1b) noise σ = 0.0717 m/s, 20 seeds (N1), against ORCA (RVO2) on the same 34; (2) noise σ = 0.07 m/s (E2) against ORCA (RVO2) on 26 second traps; (3a) noise σ* = 0.0467 m/s (E6b) against ORCA (RVO2) (E6a) on 186 seeds; (3b) the same with the independent noise stream N2; (4a, 4b) bias +b* against ORCA (legacy) and ORCA (RVO2) on 46 pair standoffs; (5a, 5b) rest-drive push + (`frozen_plus`) against ORCA (RVO2) on the lone keystones and on the second traps.

Probes and tests:
- **Freed fraction at T** (the match). Noise: the per-snapshot freed fraction p_i minus ORCA's outcome c_i ∈ {0, 1}. The effect is mean(p_i − c_i) with a 95% bootstrap interval over snapshots. The p-value comes from the paired sign-flip permutation test, exact when at most 20 differences are non-zero and otherwise 2×10^5 random flips with p = (count+1)/(B+1), so the smallest attainable p is 5×10^-6. The per-snapshot majority-rule McNemar is stored beside it. For deterministic pairs and whole runs I used exact McNemar.
- **Replay changes.** Noise: a snapshot changes when its seeds disagree on freed. Deterministic: a snapshot changes when any of its five 10^-9 m replays changes the outcome. Test: exact McNemar on the paired indicators. Whole runs have no ORCA replays, so this probe is not tested there. The two noise streams E6b and N2 disagree on completion in 65 of 186 seeds.
- **Time to free.** Censoring is handled by the restricted mean time to free up to the horizon, RMST_T: per unit, the mean over its runs of min(t_free, T), with a held run counting T. Every run is observed up to T, so RMST_T needs no censoring model. Test: paired sign-flip; bootstrap interval over units. Kaplan–Meier medians and a cluster-bootstrap interval of their difference are stored as a secondary summary.
- **Δt dependence ℓ** (E3, 20 lone-trap snapshots). On the 16 snapshots that ORCA (RVO2) frees at every Δt, the per-snapshot difference is ℓ_i(per-step noise, β_ref = 1.5 or 2.5) minus ℓ_i(ORCA) = ln t_esc(0.02)/t_esc(0.005). Test: exact sign-flip (2^16 sign patterns). E3 sets σ from β_ref, so this probe does not depend on which σ was matched; it is listed under 1a only.
- **Outcome identity** (the overlap J recast as a paired test). For a stochastic surrogate, I compared the disagreement of noise with ORCA against the disagreement of noise with independent noise, unit by unit. N1: the seed halves 0–9 and 10–19 count as independent noise (unresolved = freed in fewer than 5 of 10), and ORCA's disagreement is averaged over the two halves. Whole runs: [E6a ≠ E6b] against [N2 ≠ E6b], and [E6a ≠ N2] against [E6b ≠ N2] for 3b. For deterministic pairs, the indicator [surrogate ≠ ORCA] is tested against the replay reference [a replay ≠ the unperturbed run]. Tests: exact McNemar for 0/1 indicators and the sign-flip test for the averaged indicator.
- **Handedness** (pair). The lower-first fraction among freed standoffs, bias +b* against ORCA, by Fisher's exact test; the freed sets differ, so this comparison is unpaired. The exact binomial test against 1/2 for each arm and a McNemar test on the standoffs freed by both are stored beside it.
- **Final-state class** (whole runs, E6 only). The within-seed permutation chi-square test of `experiments/fixan/fix_a6.part_class`, 10^4 permutations, run with this script's generator. N2 has no keystone surgery, so 3b lacks this probe.
- Holm step-down correction (`revlib.holm`) over the probes within each comparison. "Separates" means Holm-adjusted p < 0.05. Bootstrap: 10^4 resamples, percentile, `default_rng(20261110)`.
- **Kind.** "Definitional" means the surrogate's side of the difference follows from how it is built, and only ORCA's side is measured. This covers replay (independent seeds against a deterministic run), ℓ (per-step noise has k_BT_eff ∝ 1/Δt by Eq. 9) and handedness (the bias sign picks the favoured member). Everything else is "empirical". For two deterministic perturbations, replay equality is measured and is empirical.

## Which "separates" claims of Sect. 6 survive

1. **Lone keystone, noise against ORCA (RVO2): "Replay and time to free separate the two."** The claim survives at both noise levels.
   - Replay: 9:0 changing snapshots (E2), Holm p = 0.0078; 14:0 (N1), Holm p = 3.7×10^-4. Definitional.
   - Time to free: RMST difference +19 s [+9.1, +29] (E2), Holm p = 0.0016; +19 s [+11, +27] (N1), Holm p = 1.0×10^-4. Empirical. The Kaplan–Meier medians are 19.8 s against 1.5 s.
   - The freed fraction does not separate them: −0.012 [−0.10, +0.08], p = 0.90 (E2); −0.018 [−0.07, +0.04], p = 0.61 (N1).
2. **Lone keystone: "the overlap does not separate them at the lone trap."** Survives. Noise disagrees with ORCA in 4.4% of snapshots against 2.9% for noise with noise. The excess is +0.015 [−0.044, +0.088], p = 1.
3. **Time-step signature: ORCA (RVO2) shows no ℓ while per-step noise does.** Survives. The paired ℓ difference is +2.7 [+2.3, +3.0] on 16 snapshots, exact p = 3.1×10^-5, Holm p = 1.2×10^-4. Definitional on the noise side; ORCA's |ℓ| ≤ 0.018 is the measured part.
4. **Whole runs: "the overlap does not separate them."** Survives. The McNemar identity test gives 33:39, p = 0.56 (E6b); 33:26, p = 0.44 (N2).
5. **Whole runs: "Time to completion and the final state separate them."** Survives.
   - Time: RMST_600 difference +72 s [+33, +110], Holm p = 0.0015; with N2, +83 s [+48, +119], Holm p = 4.5×10^-5. ORCA is faster on all 64 seeds that both complete.
   - Final-state class: within-seed permutation p = 8.0×10^-4, Holm p = 0.0024.
   - The completion match holds under McNemar (35:37, p = 0.91; N2 24:35, p = 0.19).
6. **Pair standoff: "Overlap and handedness separate bias and ORCA."** Survives for both solvers.
   - Outcome identity: legacy 17:0 discordant snapshots against the replay reference, Holm p = 7.6×10^-5; RVO2 15:0, Holm p = 3.1×10^-4. Empirical.
   - Handedness: lower member first 22/22 (bias) against 11/21 (ORCA, legacy), Fisher Holm p = 7.4×10^-4; 8/8 against 2/7 (RVO2), Holm p = 0.028. Definitional on the bias side.
   - Caveat: on the 13 legacy standoffs that both free, ORCA's first member is the bias-favoured lower one in 10 (paired McNemar 3:0, p = 0.25). The handedness difference comes from the standoffs that only one of the two frees.
   - The RVO2 handedness result rests on 8 and 7 freed standoffs.
   - Freed fraction, replay and time to free do not separate them (all p ≥ 0.86).
7. **Sect. 4.1: "J separates two perturbations only at the pair standoff, where both are deterministic."** Survives. The identity test separates only at the pair (and for the push at the second trap, a freed-fraction difference). It does not separate noise from ORCA at the lone keystone or in whole runs.
8. **Push (class L reference) against ORCA (RVO2) at the lone keystone.** Freed fraction (1:0, p = 1), identity and replay do not separate them, as the class-L reading needs.

## Findings that change or qualify claims (flag)

- **Time to free separates the class-L reference push from ORCA (RVO2) at the lone keystone.** The push frees earlier on all 30 snapshots that both free, by 0.18–4.7 s (median 0.34 s). The paired sign-flip p is at the floor, 5×10^-6 (Holm p = 2×10^-5). ORCA's own drive part matches ORCA to within 0.62 s, median 0. The paper uses time to free as a separator ("Replay and time to free separate the two", Sect. 6.1) under the rule "The two share a mechanism only if no probe separates them" (Sect. 4.2). Under paired tests, that same rule would also assign the push and ORCA different mechanisms. The reason is that a paired test of two deterministic perturbations has unlimited power against any consistent difference. Time to free is a descriptive signature (Sect. 4.1 says so). It cannot decide class membership without an effect-size threshold. Proposed fix: decide class by the Table 2 probes (replay, Δt, push ±, tilt ±b, corrected implementation) and report time to free and J as descriptive differences with their effect sizes. Alternatively, keep time to free but state the scale: +19 s for noise against −0.34 s (median) for the push.
- **The second-trap match does not hold for σ = 0.07, and nothing separates noise from ORCA there.** Noise frees 0.90 of second-trap runs against ORCA's 0.65: paired difference +0.25 [+0.07, +0.43], raw p = 0.021, Holm p = 0.062 (majority McNemar 7:1, p = 0.070). Replay (5:0) cannot reach p < 0.05, because the smallest two-sided McNemar p with 5 discordant snapshots is 0.0625. The paper makes no separation claim for noise at the second trap, so no sentence changes. The surrogate is also not fraction-matched there, and the paper does not claim it is.
- **Push at the second trap.** The push frees 8:0 more blockers than ORCA (RVO2), Holm p = 0.023, consistent with "ORCA (RVO2) frees fewer blockers than its drive part alone" (Sect. 6.1.4).

## Draft text for the paper

Protocol step 5 (replaces "A probe separates two perturbations when their 95% intervals ... exact counts."):
> A probe separates two perturbations when a paired test of their difference on the same snapshots or spawn seeds rejects equality at the 0.05 level after Holm's correction over the probes of that comparison. Class is decided by the probes of Table 2; the time to free, the overlap and the final-state class are reported with their paired effect sizes.

Statistics (Sect. 3, addition):
> Paired differences between two perturbations are tested by the exact McNemar test for paired outcomes, a paired sign-flip permutation test for per-snapshot freed fractions, the restricted mean time to free up to the horizon $T$ (RMST$_T$, a held run counting $T$) and $\ell$, Fisher's exact test for the side of the first exit, and a within-seed permutation test for the final-state class; Holm's correction acts over the probes of each comparison.
