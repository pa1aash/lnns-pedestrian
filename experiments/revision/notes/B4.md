# B4 Contact-arch convergence in the time step

**Done.** experiments/revision/B4_arch_dt.py (`analyse` -> data/derived/rev_B4.json); raw data/revision/B4/b4.jsonl.
The hard-driven arch setting of experiments/arch/arch.py (C1, CF, w = 0.8 m, 100 agents, mean desired speed 5 m/s,
up to 600 s), seeds 42–51, at Δt = 0.01, 0.0025 and 0.001 s (30 runs), with arch.py's arch definition (every 0.1 s, a
connected component of the body-overlap graph near the door that touches both jambs; persistent = lifetime ≥ 10 s).
Recorded per arch: size, the summed compression k Σ(r_ij − d_ij) (arch.py's quantity, k = 1.2 × 10⁵ kg/s²), the
largest single contact force k(r_ij − d_ij), the largest agent-agent and agent-jamb overlaps; per run also the
largest overlap among all agents. At Δt = 0.01 s the ten runs reproduce data/arch/arch.jsonl (evacuation times and
episode counts identical). 0.95 CPU-h (wall_s summed).

**Result.** Δt = 0.0025 and 0.001 s agree, and Δt = 0.01 s does not. Δt = 0.01 / 0.0025 / 0.001 s: runs with an
arch 9 / 10 / 10; episodes 69 / 409 / 404 (54 of the 69 in one run); largest arch 10 / 11 / 13 agents; peak summed
compression 75 / 18 / 18 kN; peak single contact force 31 / 4.8 / 4.8 kN; largest overlap in an arch 26 / 4.0 / 4.0
cm, at a jamb 23 / 6.1 / 6.2 cm, among all agents 35 / 6.9 / 7.0 cm; jamb contact force 28 / 7.3 / 7.4 kN;
longest lifetime 2.9 / 2.3 / 2.4 s, 95th percentile 0.66 / 1.0 / 1.1 s; median evacuation time 78 / 61 / 59 s.
The median over runs of the per-run peak compression is 10 kN at every Δt, so only the extremes are
under-resolved at 0.01 s: there single overlaps reach one radius, arches form a sixth as often, and most live
shorter (95th percentile 0.66 against 1.1 s). No arch lasts 10 s at any Δt (0 of 10 runs each, upper Wilson bound 0.28), and all 30 runs complete. The
qualitative claim (no contact arch persists) holds at every Δt. The magnitudes must change. The paper's 108 kN
(ArchPeakForce) is A13's Δt = 0.01 s sum over 10 contacts with a 24 cm overlap; at a converged step the summed
compression peaks at 18 kN, the single contact force at 4.8 kN, overlaps at 4 cm. ArchEpisodes (103),
ArchRunsWithArch (22 of 25), ArchPNinetyFive (0.6 s), ArchEvacMedian (77 s) and ArchMaxLife (2.9 s) are also
Δt = 0.01 s values. The Δt = 0.0025 s numbers (RunNFiveFine: 1060 episodes in 25 runs, 42 per run against 41 here,
22.2 kN, longest 3.3 s) agree with the converged values and should become the headline set.
