# A12: CroMa comparison (Sect. 7)

Script: `experiments/revision/A12_croma.py` (reuses the passage, flow, gap and ratio functions of
`experiments/fixan/fix_a7.py` unchanged). Output: `data/derived/rev_A12.json`. Registry: `experiments/revision/notes/A12.yaml` (59 macros, prefix `RevAtwelve`). No simulations were run; the script reads the stored E10 trajectories (about 9 s CPU).
Cross-check: the flow ratios match `fix_a7.json` exactly and the uncensored longest-gap means match `E10_analysis.json` exactly (maximum deviation 0).

Variant keys: SFM = `C1_cf`; SFM with jamb range B_w,edge = `C1_cf_edge`; force-coupled ORCA (RVO2) = `C3_rvo2_cf`; SFM + noise sigma* = `C1_cf_sig`. The JSON also holds `C1_pub` (published parameters, not in Sect. 7). Run end: a deadlocked run stops once every active agent has had |v| < 0.01 m/s for 10 s with no exit; any other incomplete run stops at the 600 s cap.

## 3. Longest gap with the terminal gap right-censored

Definition: G is the longest time between successive passages in a run. In an incomplete run the gap after the last passage is still open at the run end, so G >= c = max(longest interior gap, t_end − last passage). An incomplete run therefore enters as censored at c, and a complete run enters as an event at its longest interior gap. The uncensored mean is the paper's EtenXGap, the interior gaps only.

| variant | uncensored mean | censored (deadlock / capped) | terminal gap is the longest | terminal open gap, mean [min, max] | mean of c (lower bound) | median of c | KM median | KM restricted mean to tau (S(tau)) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SFM | 6.6 s | 24 (24 / 0) | 24 | 18 s [14, 33] | 17 s | 16 s | not reached (> 33 s) | 32 s to 33 s (0.96) |
| SFM, B_w,edge | 6.2 s | 8 (8 / 0) | 8 | 15 s [14, 17] | 9 s | 6 s | 6.5 s | 10 s to 17 s (0.32) |
| force-coupled ORCA (RVO2) | 5.7 s | 15 (0 / 15) | 15 | 444 s [423, 468] | 269 s | 433 s | not reached (> 468 s) | 283 s to 468 s (0.60) |
| SFM + noise sigma* | 72 s | 11 (0 / 11) | 9 | 322 s [2.4, 445] | 197 s | 96 s | 96 s | 226 s to 445 s (0.44) |
| humans | 1.7 s | 0 | – | – | 1.7 s | – | – | – |

Every KM restricted mean whose largest observation is censored (SFM, ORCA) is a lower bound. For the deadlocked runs the terminal gap is unbounded, because the rest state is absorbing, and the 10 s detector window cuts it short. Their censored value of 14–33 s is set by the stopping rule, not by the dynamics.

## Flags: claims in Sect. 7 that change

1. **The longest-gap sentence understates force-coupled ORCA by two orders of magnitude.** Paper: "a mean longest gap of \EtenCThreeGap--\EtenCOneGap\,s for the deterministic variants and \EtenCSigGap\,s with noise". In 15 of 25 ORCA runs no agent passes during the last 423–468 s (mean 444 s) before the 600 s cap. The terminal open gap is the longest gap in all 15. Counting it, the median longest gap is at least 433 s and the KM median is not reached by 468 s. The "5.7 s" counts only interior gaps. For the SFM, the 6.6 s is the longest gap before the deadlock, and in all 24 deadlocked runs the terminal gap (at least 14–33 s, unbounded in fact) exceeds it. The same applies to the noise variant: its 72 s is an interior-only mean, while the censored median is 96 s and the restricted mean is at least 226 s.
2. **"SFM+ORCA (RVO2) never deadlocks but completes only \EtenCThreeComplete\ runs; the other \EtenCThreeCapped\ still move at 600 s."** This is true under the detector (|v| < 0.01 m/s), but the 2–4 agents left in each capped run are stalled. In two runs checked (seeds 42 and 43), the remaining agents sit at x ≈ 9.5–9.8 m (entrance plane x = 10 m) for the last 200 s. Their x-range in that window is 0.05–0.2 m, while the frame-to-frame speed averages 0.10–0.29 m/s. That is jitter in place. They are stalls in the same sense as the noise variant's "deadlocks into stalls". Sect. 7.4 says this only of noise ("noise at σ* turns deadlocks into stalls"). It applies equally to the ORCA velocity filter.
3. Minor wording: "Participants walked normally or hurried" omits 4D130, which is h2 "full commitment" (pushing, lined up at the bottleneck). The sentence "in four runs kept a 2 m semicircle ... clear" is consistent with the data (4D030, 4D040, 4D110, 4D120).
4. The flow claims stand. The range \FixAsevenCSigFlowRatio–\FixAsevenCThreeFlowRatio = 0.40–0.59 is confirmed, and every one of the 100 model runs is below its human run.
