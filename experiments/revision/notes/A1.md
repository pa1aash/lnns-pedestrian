# A1 Identity check (rest drive of force-coupled ORCA = SFM with a shorter relaxation time)

**Done.** With no binding constraint ORCA returns v_pref = v0 ê, and the drive part of force-coupled ORCA is
w_o m(v0 ê − v)/τ_orca. Hence F_des + w_o F_drive = m(v_eff/τ + w_o v0/τ_orca) ê − m(1/τ + w_o/τ_orca) v; for w_o = 1
this is the SFM drive with τ' = τ τ_orca/(τ + τ_orca) and target speed (v_eff/τ + v0/τ_orca) τ' (= v0 when v_eff = v0).
Script experiments/revision/A1_identity.py. Two substitutions on C1: "tau" (τ → τ', v0 unchanged) and "exact"
(τ → τ', v0' chosen so that W(ρ) v0'/τ' equals the drive coefficient at the snapshot densities, including w_o).
(1) Forces on all 60 E2 snapshots against C3 with w2_orca_split = drive_only; (2) 120 s quenches of all 60 snapshots
(CF, Δt = 0.01 s, stationary stop as E2), paired with E2's drive-part arm.

**Result.** Forces: exact substitution matches to 1.7e-13 N at rest (machine precision) and to 0.04 N (relative 7e-4) at
random velocities |v| ≈ 0.3 m/s (the residual is w_o − 1 in the damping); τ-only substitution to 0.22 N (relative 1.1e-3)
at rest. Quenches: both substitutions free exactly the blockers the drive part frees — 30 of 34 lone keystones and 24 of 26
second traps, discordant 0:0 (McNemar p = 1), exit times equal to within 0.04 s (τ-only) and 0.01 s (exact; median
difference 0). τ ranges 0.4–0.6 s, so τ' = 0.22–0.27 s and the rest drive m v0/τ' is 1.8–2.2 times the SFM drive m v0/τ.
