## Pipeline
1. `experiments/hero/export_hero.py` (plan interpreter, `PYTHONPATH=code`; physics in `hero_phys.py`; colour tables from `make_luts.py`) -> `data/derived/hero/{lone.npz, pair.npz, inset.json, hero_physics.json, luts.npz, floor_deltaE.json}` (about 10 s). `select_pair.py` -> `pair_selection.json`.
2. `experiments/hero/render_hero.py` (Blender, Cycles, Metal GPU, `-t 2`) renders the image with no text, arrows, lines or scale bars and writes the projected anchors to `figures/hero/fig1_overlay.json`.
3. `tools/make_fig1_overlay.py` -> `figures/fig1_overlay.tex` (TikZ: arrows, coordinate lines, scale cluster, ribbon axes, labels; rev. 1: no inset). `tools/check_fig1_labels.py` checks every compiled word sits on light pixels (mean >= 0.70, 5th percentile >= 0.45); v2: 0 flagged.
4. `experiments/hero/final.sh` (memory gate 5.5 GB, polls every 5 min up to 4 h; `--width-px 2882 --samples 128`, Blender `-t 2`, Metal) renders and writes the overlay; `final_selfcheck/build.sh` is the 900 px self-check (overlay, compile, CVD variants with `cvd_variants.py`). `mock.sh` is the v2 mock wrapper.

## Honesty note for the text
- Heights are stylised: agents 0.15 m, kerbs 0.10 m; ribbon thickness and extrusion depth carry no data; ball and ghost radii are display sizes.
- **Ribbons (lone).** The ribbons show the energy with the neighbours pinned at the snapshot (own drive: blocker's drive + wall + the neighbours' push; added drive: the same plus ORCA's rest drive). Both balls sit at the frozen position, which is the own-drive minimum. The blocker's alone barrier (27.96 J, gated 27.90 J) is the one quoted in the text for the stuck-alone test; the pinned-neighbour barrier drawn here is 21.27 J. ORCA alone does not free this blocker (C3 rest margin +10.8 N) but does with the neighbours' push (E2: freed 6/6, 2.49 s).
- The ribbons are not the 2-D string-method minimum-energy paths: they are 1-D cuts along the door axis at fixed lateral position, and the pinned neighbours do not move.
- Pair ribbon coordinate: the common lateral shift c of both members, minimised over the opposed shift and both forward positions, relative to c = 0; "tilted" is the same coordinate with the goal direction tilted by b = 0.035 (tilt term frozen at the snapshot's perpendicular); b = 0.0315 is the fold.
- Energy scales differ between the two pairs of ribbons (35 J/cm and 1.5 J/cm); heights are comparable only within a pair.
- The thermal band is the per-step-noise effective temperature k_BT_eff = 10.2 J at sigma = 0.07 m/s (0.48 of the 21.27 J barrier drawn), not a measured fluctuation of the frozen state.
- The ghost balls mark where the ribbon leads, not simulated trajectories. The ribbons are rest-state (quasi-static) energies; E2's freeing of the lone keystone is dynamic and needs the neighbours.
- Arrows are the door-axis components, to scale: drive 131.2 N (0.94 cm), push 17.5 N (0.13 cm), ORCA 135.4 N (0.97 cm), wall 148.6 N (1.07 cm; equals drive + push).
