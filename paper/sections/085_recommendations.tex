\section{Recommendations for Modellers}\label{sec:recommend}
The open framework deadlock-screen needs a steering model with a restartable state, deterministic replay and switches for single force terms (\url{https://github.com/pa1aash/deadlock-screen}). Quench and replay are generic; the other probes need a second implementation (corrected implementation), the mechanism's force at rest (push), a goal-directed drive (tilt) or a noise term (time step). Before reading a narrow-door deadlock count as crowd behaviour, a modeller should:
\begin{enumerate}
\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})}
\item compare $g_{\max}(r)$ with the rest drive $m v_{\rm eff}/\tau$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:stuck}) over radii and speeds;
\item round segment ends (Sect.~\ref{sec:pair}) and check the wall range (Sect.~\ref{sec:lone});
\item report ORCA results per solver and per coupling (Sects.~\ref{sec:identity} and~\ref{sec:att-lone}--\ref{sec:velorca});
\item treat per-step noise, used against clogs~\cite{xu2021}, as a $\Delta t$-dependent temperature (Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff});
\item test completion, flow and censored passage gaps against experiments (Sect.~\ref{sec:validity}).
\end{enumerate}
