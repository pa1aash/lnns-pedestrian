\section{Recommendations for Modellers}\label{sec:recommend}
The open framework deadlock-screen needs a steering model with a restartable state, deterministic replay and switches for single force terms (\url{https://github.com/pa1aash/deadlock-screen}). Quench and replay are generic; the corrected-implementation probe needs a second implementation, the rest-drive push the mechanism's force at rest, the bias a goal-directed drive and the time-step probe a noise term. To screen a narrow-door deadlock count, a modeller should:
\begin{enumerate}
\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{(\roman{enumi})}
\item compare $g_{\max}(r)$ with the rest drive $m v_{\rm eff}/\tau$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:stuck}) over radii and speeds;
\item round the door edges (segment ends of radius $r_c=0.1$\,m remove the pair standoff, Sect.~\ref{sec:pair}) and check the wall range (Sect.~\ref{sec:lone});
\item report ORCA results per solver and per coupling (Sects.~\ref{sec:identity} and~\ref{sec:att-lone}--\ref{sec:velorca});
\item treat per-step noise, reported to mitigate clogs in pedestrian simulations~\cite{xu2021}, as having a $\Delta t$-dependent temperature (Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff});
\item test completion, flow and censored gaps against experiments (Sect.~\ref{sec:validity});
\item report a density throttle on the desired speed and test with it off (Eq.~\eqref{eq:des}).
\end{enumerate}
