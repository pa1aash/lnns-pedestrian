\section{Recommendations for Modellers}\label{sec:recommend}
The open framework deadlock-screen needs a steering model with a restartable state, deterministic replay and switches for single force terms. Quench and replay are generic; the other probes need a second implementation, the mechanism's force at rest, a goal-directed drive or a noise term. To screen a narrow-door deadlock count, a modeller should: (i)~compare $g_{\max}(r)$ with the rest drive $m v_{\rm eff}/\tau$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:stuck}) over radii and speeds; (ii)~round the segment ends at the door beyond the members' overhang (at $r_c=0.1$\,m no pair standoff remains) and check the wall range; (iii)~report ORCA results per solver and per coupling (Sects.~\ref{sec:identity} and~\ref{sec:att-lone}--\ref{sec:velorca}); (iv)~treat per-step noise~\cite{xu2021} as a $\Delta t$-dependent temperature (Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff}); (v)~test completion, flow and censored gaps against experiments (Sect.~\ref{sec:validity}); (vi)~report any density throttle and test with it off (Eq.~\eqref{eq:des}).
