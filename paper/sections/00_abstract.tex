\begin{abstract}
Crowd-model steering terms are credited, on outcome alone, with clearing narrow-door deadlocks. In one social force model (wall range 0.08\,m) at a 0.8\,m door, two rest states hold the last agents. In the lone trap the door-edge wall force alone holds one agent, the blocker; half the runs complete at the critical width \RevBtwoWfifty\,m. The pair standoff, two agents abreast at sharp door edges, is the only deadlock at wall range 0.04\,m beside the door, where \EsevenBjambK\ of \EsevenN\ runs complete. Optimal reciprocal collision avoidance coupled as a force (force-coupled ORCA) frees the lone trap through its goal-directed part: added to the drive, it gives the same model with relaxation time \RevAoneTauPrimeLo--\RevAoneTauPrimeHi\,s and \RevAoneDriveRatioLo--\RevAoneDriveRatioHi\ times the drive at rest. This part alone completes \RwOneDriveK\ of \RwOneN\ whole runs, against \RwOneOrcaK\ for force-coupled ORCA. As a velocity filter, ORCA frees none. Per-step noise matched in freed fraction frees deadlocks by fluctuations; a lateral bias matched in the same way tilts the pair standoff out of its minimum. Probes of saved deadlocks assign three mechanism classes; replay and the time step separate per-step noise from force-coupled ORCA. With the blocker alone, noise frees it by activated escape at barriers above 1.5 noise temperatures, with Arrhenius slope \RvAthreeRatioDeep--\RvAthreeRatioMid\ of the computed barrier, the transition-state value. A sign defect makes our earlier ORCA solver replay-sensitive. In five CroMa experiments at this width everyone passes; the uncalibrated model passes at most \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of human flow, \RvBoneCroOffMin--\RvBoneCroOffMax\ without density-dependent speed reduction.

\end{abstract}
