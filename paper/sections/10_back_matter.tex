\subsubsection*{Main Differences from the Conference Version.}
This paper extends our SIMULTECH 2026 paper~\cite{gang2026}, from which it keeps one idea: a paradigm's effect is audited against a minimal control matched to its headline score. The title, abstract, conclusions, figures and tables are new, and every quantity carried over from the conference paper was recomputed from new runs. New in this version are the five mechanism classes with their physical signatures and the rest-state lemma; the deadlock physics (the stuck-alone criterion at the door edges, the pair standoff and its fold, the absence of persistent contact arches, the exact effective temperature of per-step noise and collective activated escape over computed barriers); attribution on a common library of frozen deadlocks with paired statistics, with ORCA reported for the published solver and for RVO2; a corrected-forward model mode beside the published one; and a like-for-like comparison with human bottleneck data at 0.8\,m. The conference reading that noise and ORCA resolve deadlocks alike is replaced by the finding that they reach the same rate by different mechanisms, and the conference claims that the new results revise are listed in Sect.~\ref{sec:att-conf}.
