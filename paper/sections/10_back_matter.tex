\subsubsection*{Main Differences from the Conference Version.}
This paper extends our SIMULTECH 2026 paper~\cite{gang2026}, keeping its minimum-intervention control matched to a paradigm's headline score; every carried-over result was recomputed. New are the mechanism classes, deadlock physics, attribution on frozen deadlocks and human-data comparison; Sect.~\ref{sec:att-conf} lists the revised conference claims.
