\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
The deadlocks of this social force model at a narrow door are rest states set by the wall range and the shape of the door-edge segment ends, and with the default walls half the runs complete at $w=\RevBtwoWfifty$\,m. Force-coupled ORCA, velocity noise and a lateral bias free them through three mechanisms that reach the same freed fraction and that the probes separate. With uncalibrated default parameters, every variant in the CroMa bottleneck passes \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of the human flow. A narrow-door deadlock count can be read as crowd behaviour once probes have named its mechanism and matched experiments have tested the model's flow and passage gaps (Sect.~\ref{sec:recommend}).
