\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
% Budget 0.4 page (C54).
In a social-force bottleneck, a matched completion rate leaves the attribution open, and signatures derived from the physics of frozen deadlocks settle it. Narrow-exit deadlocks in the model are of two types: a lone agent held by the wall force at the door edges, and a pair standoff at the jamb faces, a strict minimum of the rest-state energy that a lateral bias removes; contact arches form under a hard drive but do not persist. Controls matched to ORCA's rate, noise at the lone trap and in whole runs and a lateral bias at the pair, reproduce the rate and differ from ORCA on the signatures of their own class. At the lone trap and in whole runs noise fails on deadlocks that overlap ORCA's at least as much as a stochastic resolver overlaps with itself, so identity does not separate them; replay, the time step, time to free and the final state do. At the pair the bias frees partly different standoffs and has a handedness that ORCA lacks. ORCA, added as a force, reshapes the landscape with its rest drive at the lone trap, and at the pair its constraint part acts against that drive, solver by solver. Rebuilt like for like around a human bottleneck experiment, no model variant flows like people: each deadlocks or passes \EtenCSigFlowRatio--\EtenCThreeFlowRatio\ of the human flow. A mechanism claim needs a probe of the mechanism, applied to the same deadlocks as the paradigm.
