\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
The deadlocks of this social force model at a narrow door are rest states set by the wall range and the door-edge segment ends; with the default walls half the runs complete at $w=\RevBtwoWfifty$\,m. Force-coupled ORCA frees the lone trap through its rest drive: the drive plus the drive part is this model with $\tau'=\RevAoneTauPrimeLo$--$\RevAoneTauPrimeHi$\,s and \RevAoneDriveRatioLo--\RevAoneDriveRatioHi\ times the rest drive. Noise fraction-matched to it at horizon $T$ frees deadlocks by fluctuations, and a matched lateral bias tilts the pair standoff out of its minimum; activated escape, with an Arrhenius slope \RvAthreeRatioDeep--\RvAthreeRatioMid\ of the computed barrier, is established only with the blocker alone at $\beta\ge1.5$. With uncalibrated defaults, every variant passes \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of the human flow over whole CroMa runs, and \RvBoneCroOffMin--\RvBoneCroOffMax\ without the density throttle. A narrow-door deadlock count should not be read as crowd behaviour until probes name its mechanism and matched experiments test its flow and passage gaps.
