\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
The deadlocks of this social force model at a narrow door are rest states set by the wall range and the door-edge segment ends; with the default walls half the runs complete at $w=\RevBtwoWfifty$\,m. Force-coupled ORCA frees the lone trap through the rest drive that its drive part adds. Noise fraction-matched to it at horizon $T$ frees deadlocks by fluctuations at the matched level, and a matched lateral bias tilts the pair standoff out of its minimum; activated escape is established only with the blocker alone at $\beta\ge1.5$. With uncalibrated default parameters, every variant passes \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of the human flow in the CroMa bottleneck. A narrow-door deadlock count should not be read as crowd behaviour until probes have named its mechanism and matched experiments have tested its flow and passage gaps.
