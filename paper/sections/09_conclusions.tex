\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
% Budget 0.4 page (C54).
A control matched to a paradigm's completion rate shows that a simpler cause would suffice, not that the paradigm uses it. In a social-force bottleneck, signatures derived from the physics of frozen deadlocks settle the attribution that a matched rate leaves open. Three findings support this. Narrow-exit deadlocks in the model come in two classes, a lone agent held by the wall force at the door edges and a symmetric pair standoff that is a strict minimum of the rest-state energy, and contact arches do not persist. Controls matched to ORCA's rate, noise at the lone trap and in whole runs and a lateral bias at the pair, reproduce the rate but free other deadlocks and differ from ORCA in replay, time-step dependence or handedness: ORCA reshapes the landscape with its rest drive at the lone trap and acts through its constraint part, solver by solver, at the pair. Rebuilt like for like around a human bottleneck experiment, no model variant flows like people: each deadlocks or passes \EtenCSigFlowRatio--\EtenCThreeFlowRatio\ of the human flow. A mechanism claim needs a test where the mechanism acts, not a second number that matches the first.
