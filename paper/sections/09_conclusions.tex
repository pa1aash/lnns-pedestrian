\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
At a 0.8\,m exit the social force model deadlocks in two ways, both set by the wall geometry at the door edges. A lone agent is held by the door-edge wall force if and only if $g_{\max}(r)>m v_0/\tau$, which brackets the critical width of \EsevenWcrit\,m under the default wall law. Under a short-range wall law a pair standoff forms at the jamb faces, a strict minimum that a lateral bias removes non-smoothly where a member reaches the end of a jamb segment. No contact arch persists. ORCA added as a force frees deadlocks through its rest drive, its rate-matched surrogates through activated escape (noise) and a tilt of the pair minimum (bias). Velocity-coupled ORCA has no rest drive and frees no deadlock (class P, Sect.~\ref{sec:velorca}). The probes separate the mechanisms, and replays expose the leg-normal sign defect of the legacy solver. In the CroMa bottleneck every model variant passes only \FixAsevenCSigFlowRatio--\FixAsevenCThreeFlowRatio\ of the human flow.
