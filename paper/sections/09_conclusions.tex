\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
% Budget 0.4 page (C54).
In a social-force bottleneck, a matched completion rate leaves the attribution open, and signatures from the physics of frozen deadlocks settle it. The narrow-door deadlocks are of two types: a lone agent held by the door-edge wall force and a pair standoff at the jamb faces that a lateral bias removes; contact arches break within \ArchMaxLife\,s. Controls matched to ORCA's rate differ from ORCA on their own class's signatures: noise fails on overlapping deadlocks but differs in replay, time-step dependence, time to free and final state; the bias differs in handedness and in the standoffs it frees. ORCA, added as a force, reshapes the lone-trap landscape with its rest drive; at the pair its constraint part acts against its drive part, differently in the two solvers. In a human bottleneck experiment rebuilt like for like, every model variant passes only \EtenCSigFlowRatio--\EtenCThreeFlowRatio\ of the human flow. A mechanism claim needs a probe of the mechanism, applied to the same deadlocks as the paradigm.
