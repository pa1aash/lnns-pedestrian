\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
The deadlocks of this social force model at a narrow door are rest states made by the door-edge geometry, and with the default walls half the runs complete at $w=\RevBtwoWfifty$\,m. Force-coupled ORCA, velocity noise and a lateral bias free them through three mechanisms that a matched freed fraction does not distinguish and the probes do. Freeing the deadlocks does not bring the model to the human data: in the CroMa bottleneck every variant passes \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of the human flow. A narrow-door deadlock count describes the wall law and steering terms until the probes have named its mechanism and matched experiments have tested its flow and gaps (Sect.~\ref{sec:recommend}).
