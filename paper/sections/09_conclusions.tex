\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
The deadlocks of this social force model at a narrow door are rest states set by the wall range and the shape of the door-edge segment ends, and with the default walls half the runs complete at $w=\RevBtwoWfifty$\,m. Velocity noise and a lateral bias fraction-matched to force-coupled ORCA at horizon $T$ free them through other mechanisms, activated escape and a lateral tilt, and the probes separate all three. With uncalibrated default parameters, every variant in the CroMa bottleneck passes \RevDThreeRatioMin--\RevDThreeRatioMax\ of the human flow. A narrow-door deadlock count can be read as crowd behaviour once probes have named its mechanism and matched experiments have tested its flow and passage gaps (Sect.~\ref{sec:recommend}).
