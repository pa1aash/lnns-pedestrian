\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}
% Budget 0.4 page (C54).
In a social-force bottleneck, a matched completion rate leaves the attribution open, and signatures from the physics of frozen deadlocks settle it. The model's narrow-door deadlocks are of two types: a lone agent held by the door-edge wall force, and a pair standoff at the jamb faces, a strict minimum of the rest-state energy that a lateral bias removes; contact arches form under a hard drive but do not persist. Controls matched to ORCA's rate reproduce it and differ from ORCA on their own class's signatures. Noise, at the lone trap and in whole runs, fails on deadlocks that overlap ORCA's at least as much as a stochastic resolver overlaps itself, so identity does not separate them; replay, the time step, time to free and the final state do. At the pair the bias frees partly different standoffs, with a handedness ORCA lacks. ORCA, added as a force, reshapes the lone-trap landscape with its rest drive; at the pair its constraint part acts against that drive, solver by solver. Rebuilt like for like around a human bottleneck experiment, every model variant passes \EtenCSigFlowRatio--\EtenCThreeFlowRatio\ of the human flow. A mechanism claim needs a probe of the mechanism, applied to the same deadlocks as the paradigm.
