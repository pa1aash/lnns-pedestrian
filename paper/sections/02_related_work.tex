\section{Related Work}\label{sec:background}
\subsubsection{Steering Models.}
The SFM relaxes each pedestrian towards a desired velocity under exponential repulsion from others and walls~\cite{helbing1995}, with body compression and sliding friction in contact~\cite{helbing2000nature}. The TTC law, inferred from trajectory statistics, makes the interaction energy fall off as $\tau_{ij}^{-2}$ in the predicted time to collision $\tau_{ij}$~\cite{karamouzas2014}. The velocity obstacle of a neighbour is the set of own velocities that lead to contact within a time horizon~\cite{fiorini1998}; ORCA shares the avoidance between the two agents and reduces each obstacle to a half-plane, so that the new velocity, found by a two-dimensional linear program, is the permitted velocity nearest to the preferred one~\cite{vandenberg2011}. Our model combines all three as force terms, so that each can be switched off at a frozen deadlock.

\subsubsection{Clogging at Bottlenecks.}
Grains, colloids, sheep and people passing a narrow door alternate between bursts of flow and clogs; the flow is clogged when the mean duration of the stoppages diverges~\cite{zuriguel2014}. In granular hoppers the clog is a frictional arch, and unclogging under vibration is escape from traps whose depths follow a distribution~\cite{nicolas2018trap}. In pedestrian models, boundary parameters alone decide whether a velocity-based model clogs~\cite{xu2021}, and the SFM forms transient blocking clusters~\cite{parisi2005microscopic}, some attached to the walls~\cite{cornes2021microscopic}. The SFM considered here has no static friction; its deadlocks are rest states of a driven, damped system.

\subsubsection{Activated Escape.}
A particle held in a potential well and kicked by thermal noise leaves the well at a rate $\lambda=\nu\exp(-\Delta U/k_BT)$, with $\Delta U$ the barrier height and $\nu$ an attempt rate~\cite{arrhenius1889,kramers1940}. H\"anggi, Talkner and Borkovec reviewed the theory, including the many-dimensional case, in which the lowest saddle between two minima sets the rate~\cite{hanggi1990}. The string method finds that saddle by evolving a discretised path until it is everywhere parallel to the force~\cite{e2002,e2007}. For noise injected into a damped velocity recursion, the stationary variance fixes an effective temperature that depends on the time step~\cite{gronbechjensen2013}; we use this dependence as a probe (Sect.~\ref{sec:teff}). The equilibrium form applies because our rest field is a gradient and the noise is additive; for self-propelled noise the rate depends on the whole shape of the barrier~\cite{woillez2020}.

\subsubsection{Deadlock in Multi-Agent Systems.}
Navigation along a potential gradient stalls in local minima of the navigation potential~\cite{koren1991}. For multi-robot systems that avoid collisions through control barrier functions, Grover et al.\ characterised deadlocks as equilibria of the closed-loop dynamics, analysed their stability and proposed perturbations to leave them~\cite{grover2020}. Symmetric configurations deadlock such robots and added asymmetry helps them escape~\cite{grover2020symmetry}, and ORCA itself deadlocks in symmetric robot scenes~\cite{liu2026orcadeadlock}. The pair standoff of Sect.~\ref{sec:pair} is the pedestrian analogue. The surrogates we test, noise and a lateral bias, are perturbations of this kind.

\subsubsection{Testing Mechanisms in Models.}
Pattern-oriented modelling asks a model to reproduce several patterns at different scales, because a single pattern rarely discriminates between mechanisms~\cite{grimm2005}. In our screening protocol the rival explanation is a rate-matched surrogate tuned to the rate of the mechanism under test, and its discriminating patterns are the responses of frozen deadlocks to probes, predicted from the physics of each mechanism class.
