\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

In this social force model force-coupled ORCA, the matched noise and the matched lateral bias free the same fraction of deadlocks at a horizon $T$ through different forces, the rest drive, fluctuations and a tilt of the pair standoff (Tables~\ref{tab:signatures} and~\ref{tab:observed}). At the lone keystone the rest-drive push responds as force-coupled ORCA does to every probe that separates the classes.

\subsubsection{Limitations.}
Agents are two-dimensional discs, 100 per room run. The lone trap originates in the default wall range ($B_w=0.08$\,m), the pair standoff in sharp segment ends with a short wall range. Force coupling is not ORCA's published form, and native ORCA is untested (Sect.~\ref{sec:identity}). The density throttle on the desired speed (Eq.~\eqref{eq:des}) is not part of the original social force model~\cite{helbing1995,helbing2000nature} and shapes the rest drive, although removing it leaves $w_{50}$ and the completions at 0.8\,m unchanged (Sect.~\ref{sec:model}); in the CroMa runs it lowers the flow (Sect.~\ref{sec:validity}).
The stuck-alone fraction and $w_{50}$ depend on the radius through $g_{\max}(r)$ (Sect.~\ref{sec:lone}).
Agents differ in temperature ($k_{\rm B}T_{\rm eff}\propto m_i\tau_i$), so the stationary state is not Boltzmann; $\beta$ uses the blocker's $\tau$, as the blocker carries most of the unstable mode (Sect.~\ref{sec:teff}).
Except for the time-step probes and arch runs, all runs use $\Delta t=0.01$\,s.
%C2: B2 dt = 0.005 s check
A fraction match holds within a window of horizons (Sect.~\ref{sec:protocol}). On the \PairN\ pair-set snapshots, 95\% intervals of freed-fraction differences reach [$\RvAfivePairLegacyDiffLo$, $\RvAfivePairLegacyDiffHi$] (Table~\ref{tab:matched-bias}). The matched noise levels lie below the tested range $\beta\ge1.5$ (Sects.~\ref{sec:escape} and~\ref{sec:att-whole}), so the whole-run stall prediction extrapolates the Arrhenius law outside the Kramers regime. Doors are 0.70--1.00\,m wide in the width runs and 0.8\,m elsewhere.
