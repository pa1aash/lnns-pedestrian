\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Matched Outcomes and Related Systems}
In this social force model a freed fraction matched at horizon $T$ is weak evidence of a shared mechanism. Force-coupled ORCA reaches it through Reshape, axial; noise through Heat; and the lateral bias through Reshape, lateral (Sect.~\ref{sec:attribution}). The rest-drive push, of its class, agrees with it on every defining probe (Table~\ref{tab:signatures}).

Here noise frees the lone trap by activated escape over an effective barrier \RevBthreeAllDeep--\RevBthreeAllMid\ of the computed one, with fitted attempt frequencies as in trap models; granular and pedestrian clogging is read through arches and clog lifetimes~\cite{zuriguel2014,nicolas2018trap}. The pair standoff is a force-balance equilibrium of the kind resolved in robots by a switching controller~\cite{grover2023}; here the lateral bias frees it past $b_f$ and noise at $\beta<1$ (Sect.~\ref{sec:pair}).

\subsection{Limitations}
Agents are two-dimensional discs, 100 per room run, and the deadlocks are those of the last agents of an evacuation. The lone trap originates in the default wall range ($B_w=0.08$\,m), the pair standoff in sharp segment ends with a short wall range (library built with $B_w=0.04$\,m). Force coupling is not ORCA's published form, and native ORCA is untested (Sect.~\ref{sec:identity}). A fraction match holds within a window of horizons (Sect.~\ref{sec:protocol}), and the \PairN\ pair snapshots give 95\% intervals as wide as [$\TabThreePairPubDiffLo$, $\TabThreePairPubDiffHi$] for differences in freed fraction (Table~\ref{tab:matched-bias}). The matched noise levels lie below the tested range $\beta\ge1.5$ (median $\beta$ \RevAthreeSevenMed\ at the lone keystone, \RevAthreeStarMed\ in whole runs), so the whole-run stall prediction extrapolates the Arrhenius law outside the Kramers regime, and the arch searches below \ArchVZero\,m/s ran at $\Delta t=0.01$\,s. The model's 2.0\,m CroMa walls are thin, the archive's taper to a wedge. All results come from door geometries near $w=0.8$\,m, the width of the human data.
