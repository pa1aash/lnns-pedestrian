\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Mechanisms Behind a Matched Freed Fraction}
In this social force model a freed fraction matched at horizon $T$ is weak evidence of a shared mechanism. Force-coupled ORCA reaches it through Reshape, axial; noise through Heat; and the lateral bias through Reshape, lateral (Sect.~\ref{sec:attribution}). The rest-drive push, of the same class as force-coupled ORCA, agrees with it on every defining probe (Table~\ref{tab:signatures}).

\subsection{Relation to Clogging and Robot Deadlock}
Here noise frees the lone trap by activated escape over the model's own barriers, with the attempt rate fitted as in trap models; granular and pedestrian clogging is read through arches and clog-lifetime statistics~\cite{zuriguel2014,nicolas2018trap}. The pair standoff is a closed-loop equilibrium of the kind Grover et al.\ resolve with a switching controller~\cite{grover2023}; here the lateral bias frees it past $b_f$ and noise at $\beta<1$ (Sect.~\ref{sec:pair}).

\subsection{Limitations}
Agents are two-dimensional discs, 100 per room run, and the deadlocks are those of the last agents of an evacuation. The lone trap originates in the default wall range ($B_w=0.08$\,m) and the pair standoff in sharp segment ends: the pair library was built with $B_w=0.04$\,m on every wall, and rounded segment ends ($r_c=0.1$\,m) remove the standoff. Force coupling is not ORCA's published form; native ORCA, which sets velocities, treats walls as obstacles and has no wall social force, is untested. A fraction match holds within a window of horizons (from \RevAeightLKNoiseCMatchFrom\,s at the lone keystone, from \RevAeightWholeNoiseMatchFrom\,s in whole runs), and the \PairN\ pair snapshots give 95\% intervals as wide as [\TabThreePairPubDiffLo, \TabThreePairPubDiffHi] for differences in freed fraction (Table~\ref{tab:matched-bias}). The whole-run stall prediction extrapolates the Arrhenius law below the tested range $\beta\ge1.5$, outside the Kramers regime, and the arch searches below \ArchVZero\,m/s ran at $\Delta t=0.01$\,s. The model's 2.0\,m CroMa bottleneck is straight; the experimental one widens in its second metre. All results come from one family of door geometries near $w=0.8$\,m, the width of the human data.
