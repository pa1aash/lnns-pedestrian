\section{Rest States, Noise Temperature and Barriers}\label{sec:methods}
\subsection{Rest States and Their Stability}\label{sec:rest}
With densities frozen, the $n$ residual agents, stacked into $\mathbf{x},\mathbf{v}\in\mathbb R^{2n}$, have the rest field $\mathbf{F}_0(\mathbf{x})=\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{0})$, and the rest states (fixed points) are the states $(\mathbf{x}^\ast,\mathbf{0})$ with $\mathbf{F}_0(\mathbf{x}^\ast)=\mathbf{0}$. Where $\mathbf{F}_0=-\nabla U$ and the remainder dissipates, $\mathbf{v}\cdot[\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{v})-\mathbf{F}_0(\mathbf{x})]\le0$, every strict local minimum of $U$ is Lyapunov stable by the Lagrange--Dirichlet theorem~\cite{merkin1996}, with Lyapunov function $\tfrac12\mathbf{v}^{\top}\mathsf{M}\mathbf{v}+U$ and $\mathsf{M}$ the mass matrix. For SFM+TTC the dissipation condition holds only below the TTC cutoff of $10^{-4}$\,m/s, so there the stability of a snapshot rests on the quench.

In SFM friction dissipates and vanishes at rest; the rest field is $-\nabla U$ (to \ResidAllMax\,N) with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:U}
U(\mathbf{x})=\sum_i c_i\,|\mathbf{x}_i-\mathbf{g}|+\sum_{i<j}\phi_{ij}(d_{ij})+\sum_{i,W}\phi_{iW}(d_{iW}),\qquad c_i=\frac{m_iv_{\mathrm{eff},i}}{\tau_i},
\end{equation}
$\phi$ the exponential and contact potentials. The Hessian of $U$ is positive definite at all \RevAtenStrict\ screening-set snapshots, so every one is a strict minimum. The ORCA term at $\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{0}$ enters $\mathbf{F}_0$ as a drive part $m_iv_{0,i}\hat{\mathbf e}_i/\tau_{\rm orca}$, added to the rest drive, and a non-conservative constraint part (Sect.~\ref{sec:att-pair}).

\subsection{Barriers from the String Method}\label{sec:barrier}
The string method~\cite{e2007}, with its highest image refined to the saddle by the climbing-image step of~\cite{henkelman2000}, computes the minimum-energy path from the snapshot minimum to an exit, with the blocker at $x=\RvAeightXExit$\,m, in three conditions: all residual agents mobile (full), neighbours pinned (pinned) or the blocker alone (alone). The barrier $\Delta U$ is the largest rise along the path, $\max_{i<j}(U_j-U_i)$ over images $i<j$ (the energy span). It equals the saddle height above the snapshot minimum except on \RxSpanAffFull\ of the \RxSpanBarN\ full-condition paths, which first pass a deeper rest state in which the neighbours have moved and the blocker is still upstream; there the climb out of that rest state sets the rate. Pinned and alone paths have no such state. A barrier is accepted when the string passes four convergence tests (perpendicular force, image doubling, climbing-image gradient and saddle overlap) and, in the alone condition, agrees with a grid path; all \RvAeightFailed\ rejected strings are second traps. A noise quench of the time-step or Arrhenius set ends when the blocker's centre crosses the door plane $x=10$\,m and is censored at 300\,s (900\,s for $\beta=3.5$ and for per-step noise at $\Delta t=0.02$\,s). Kramers theory~\cite{kramers1940,langer1969,hanggi1990} predicts exponential times to free (Weibull shape 1) and $\ln\lambda$ of slope $-\Delta U$ in $1/k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}$.

\subsection{Effective Temperature of Per-Step Noise}\label{sec:teff}
Away from contact, with frozen densities, linear friction and no speed clipping, a velocity component of agent $i$ follows $v_{n+1}=(1-\Delta t/\tau_i)\,v_n+\xi_n$, $\langle\xi_n\xi_{n'}\rangle=\sigma^2\delta_{nn'}$, and $m_i\langle v^2\rangle=k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:teff}
k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{m_i\,\sigma^2}{1-(1-\Delta t/\tau_i)^2}=\frac{m_i\,\sigma^2\tau_i}{2\,\Delta t}\left(1-\frac{\Delta t}{2\tau_i}\right)^{-1},
\end{equation}
$\propto1/\Delta t$ at fixed per-step $\sigma$ up to the last factor, which alone remains for $\sqrt{\Delta t}$-scaled noise. Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff} uses the relaxation time $\tau_i$ of the agent it describes, so agents differ in temperature ($\tau_i$ spreads over 0.4--0.6\,s). The time-step and Arrhenius probes set $\sigma$ per snapshot from the blocker's $\tau_i$ for a given $\beta=\Delta U/k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}$ at $\Delta t=0.01$\,s. Weighting each agent's $k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}$ by its share of the unstable mode at the saddle changes the full-condition temperature by factors \RvAsevenCMin--\RvAsevenCMax\ (median \RvAsevenCMed), because the blocker carries most of that mode; in the pinned and alone conditions only the blocker moves.
At fixed per-step $\sigma$, $\beta\propto\Delta t$ up to the last factor of Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff}. A time-step probe set to $\beta_0=1.5$ or 2.5 at $\Delta t=0.01$\,s (\RvAfourNLevel\ snapshots each) therefore spans $\beta=\RvAfourPerStepMin$--\RvAfourPerStepMax\ over $\Delta t=0.005$--0.02\,s, while $\sqrt{\Delta t}$-scaled noise stays at $\beta=\RvAfourScaledMin$--\RvAfourScaledMax. If $\lambda\propto\exp(-\Delta U/k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}})$, the log rate ratio of $\Delta t_1=0.005$ and $\Delta t_2=0.02$\,s,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ell}
\ell\equiv\ln\frac{\lambda(\Delta t_1)}{\lambda(\Delta t_2)}=\Delta U\left(\frac{1}{k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}(\Delta t_2)}-\frac{1}{k_{\mathrm{B}}T_{\mathrm{eff}}(\Delta t_1)}\right),
\end{equation}
has no free parameter: positive at fixed per-step $\sigma$, zero up to the last factor of Eq.~\eqref{eq:teff} for $\sqrt{\Delta t}$-scaled noise and absent for a deterministic resolver. Force-coupled ORCA is deterministic, so its time-step probe compares one unperturbed quench per $\Delta t$, with the inverse time to free $1/t_{\rm esc}$ in place of $\lambda$.

\subsection{Statistics}\label{sec:stats}
Intervals are 95\% and unadjusted: Wilson for proportions, Kaplan--Meier~\cite{kaplanmeier1958} for times, Student $t$ for CroMa means, Welch $t$ for CroMa log flow ratios and otherwise a bootstrap over snapshots with all their seeds. McNemar's binomial test compares paired outcomes and Fisher's exact test unpaired counts. A paired sign-flip permutation test compares freed fractions per snapshot and times to free by their restricted mean up to $T$ (RMST)~\cite{royston2013}. The overlap $\Omega=|A_1\cap A_2|/|A_1\cup A_2|$ is the Jaccard index of two perturbations' unresolved sets $A_1$, $A_2$ (freed sets at the pair standoff), with a hypergeometric $p$-value. Outcome identity compares how often two perturbations disagree per snapshot or seed with how often two independent runs of the surrogate (or replays of the deterministic arm) disagree. {} at the lone keystones four (freed fraction, replay, RMST and either $\ell$ or outcome identity), at the second traps three, in whole runs four (freed fraction, outcome identity, RMST and final-state class) and at the pair standoff five per solver (freed fraction, replay, outcome identity, RMST and first exit). The final-state class of a failed whole run records whether it ends at rest or still moving at 600\,s and the keystone class of its held blocker. $p$-values and derived quantities carry two significant figures (three where an interval or a threshold needs them), and $p$-values below $10^{-3}$ are given as a power-of-ten upper bound.
