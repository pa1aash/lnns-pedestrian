\paragraph{Steering paradigms.}
The SFM writes the motion of each pedestrian as Newton's equation with a relaxation towards a desired velocity and exponential repulsion from other pedestrians and walls~\cite{helbing1995}; the extension used for crowd crushes adds a body-compression force and a sliding friction that act only in contact~\cite{helbing2000nature}. The TTC law of Karamouzas et al.\ was inferred from trajectory statistics: the interaction energy depends on the predicted time to collision $\tau$ and falls off as $\tau^{-2}$ with an exponential cut-off~\cite{karamouzas2014}. Velocity-obstacle methods take a geometric route. The velocity obstacle of a neighbour is the set of own velocities that lead to contact within a time horizon~\cite{fiorini1998}; ORCA shares the avoidance between the two agents and reduces each obstacle to a half-plane so that the new velocity is the solution of a two-dimensional linear program nearest to the preferred velocity~\cite{vandenberg2011}. The reference implementation is the RVO2 library~\cite{vandenberg2011rvo2}. Sect.~\ref{sec:model} combines all three in one force-based model, so that single terms can be switched off at a frozen state.

\paragraph{Clogging at bottlenecks.}
Grains in a silo, colloids, sheep and people passing a narrow door all alternate between bursts of flow and clogs. Zuriguel et al.\ proposed that the flow is clogged when the mean duration of the stoppages diverges, a property of the heavy tail of their distribution~\cite{zuriguel2014}. In granular hoppers the clog is an arch held by contact forces and friction; vibration breaks it. Nicolas, Garcimart\'in and Zuriguel described unclogging under vibration as escape from traps whose depths are spread over a distribution, which accounts for broad clog-time statistics without invoking a single barrier~\cite{nicolas2018trap}. The SFM considered here has no static friction, so it cannot hold a frictional arch. Its deadlocks are rest states of a driven, damped system, and we ask what each intervention does to those rest states.

\paragraph{Activated escape.}
A particle held in a potential well and kicked by thermal noise leaves the well at a rate $\lambda=\nu\exp(-\Delta U/k_BT)$, where $\Delta U$ is the barrier height and $\nu$ an attempt frequency~\cite{arrhenius1889,kramers1940}; H\"anggi, Talkner and Borkovec reviewed the theory, including the many-dimensional case in which the rate is set by the lowest saddle between two minima~\cite{hanggi1990}. In many dimensions the barrier is found along a minimum-energy path. The string method evolves a discretised path until it is everywhere parallel to the force~\cite{e2002,e2007}. Our noise is injected into a damped velocity recursion, and its effective temperature follows exactly from that recursion (Sect.~\ref{sec:teff}).

\paragraph{Deadlock in multi-agent systems.}
Gradient-based navigation has long been known to stall in local minima of the navigation potential~\cite{koren1991}. In multi-robot systems that avoid collisions through control barrier functions, Grover et al.\ characterised deadlocks as equilibria of the closed-loop dynamics, analysed their stability and proposed perturbation strategies to leave them~\cite{grover2020}; their safety constraints play the role of ORCA's half-planes here. The controls we test, noise and a lateral bias, are the generic perturbations used to leave such equilibria; the question of which one a given resolver resembles is the same question in both fields.

\paragraph{Testing mechanisms in models.}
Pattern-oriented modelling asks that a model reproduce several patterns observed at different scales, because a single pattern rarely discriminates between candidate mechanisms~\cite{grimm2005}. Our protocol shares the premise but differs in two ways. The rival explanation is a control fitted to the paradigm's own headline score, and the discriminating patterns are interventional responses of a frozen deadlock, predicted from the physics of each mechanism class before they are measured. We have not found an earlier study that tests a matched control in this way.
