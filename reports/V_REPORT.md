**Draft §6.4** (\subsection{Velocity-Coupled ORCA}):
With velocity coupling ORCA adds no force. After the force update its linear program returns the velocity nearest the proposed one, $\mathbf v'=\Pi(\mathbf v+\mathbf F\Delta t/m)$, under the neighbour half-planes, and the gate $w_o$ weights the change. At a frozen deadlock no pair is in contact and every half-plane leaves a slack of at least $g/2\tau_h$ at $\mathbf v'\approx\mathbf 0$. The program therefore returns $\mathbf v'$, the coupling has no rest drive, and Proposition~\ref{prop:rest} predicts class P. On the \VelLoneSnaps\ lone-trap snapshots it frees \VelLoneRvoK\ of \VelLoneN\ runs with the RVO2 solver and \VelLonePubK\ with the legacy linear-program solver (95\% upper bounds \VelLoneRvoHi\ and \VelLonePubHi). Force coupling frees \VelLoneForceRvoK\ and \VelLoneForcePubK\ ($\Delta=\VelLoneRvoDiff$ [\VelLoneRvoDiffLo, \VelLoneRvoDiffHi], $p=\VelLoneRvoP$). On the \VelPairSnaps\ pair snapshots velocity coupling frees \VelPairRvoK\ and \VelPairPubK\ of \VelPairN, against \VelPairForceRvoK\ and \VelPairForcePubK\ with force coupling. Each run repeats the SFM restart bit for bit (\VelLoneIdentRvo\ of \VelLoneN; \VelPairIdentRvo\ of \VelPairN), and no replay changes an outcome. Over the \VelWholeN\ seeds of Sect.~\ref{sec:att-whole}, \VelWholeK\ runs complete [\VelWholeLo, \VelWholeHi]; SFM completes \VelWholeSfmK\ (discordant seeds \VelWholeOnlyVel\ and \VelWholeOnlySfm) and force coupling \VelWholeForceK\ ($p=\VelWholeForceP$). Each of the \VelWholeFail\ failures ends at rest in a state that SFM (\VelWholeHeldSfm) and velocity-coupled ORCA (\VelWholeHeldOwn) both hold: \VelWholeLoneK\ lone keystones and \VelWholeSecondK\ with a second trap. Velocity-coupled ORCA is class P and leaves the rate of deadlock formation unchanged at this sample size. ORCA resolves deadlocks under force coupling through its rest drive.

**Flag semantics for R to port.** `w2_orca_coupling` ∈ {"force" (default, unchanged), "velocity"}. "velocity" requires `w2_numerics="fixed"`, `w2_orca_split="full"` and `w2_orca_transform="none"` (otherwise ValueError), and only acts in configurations that contain ORCA. With "velocity" the ORCA term is dropped from F. Each step:
1. v' = Π(v + FΔt/m); add noise; apply Π again (Π caps the speed at 2v0).
2. For each active agent, v_o = LP(preferred = v'). The half-planes come from the start-of-step x and v, the same neighbours within 3 m, horizon τh and collision dt. The solver is set by `w2_orca` (rvo2: nearest-first RVO2 lines + LP2/LP3; published: the legacy builder + solve_2d_lp); the speed circle is 2v0.
3. v' ← v' + w_o(ρ)(Π(v_o) − v'), with w_o = 1 − σ(ρ; 4, 2). Inactive agents are unchanged.
4. x' = x + v'Δt.
