# Paper and deadlock-screen: name alignment

Read-only check, 2026-11-10. Neither repository was edited.

- Paper: `paper/sections/*.tex`, `paper/tables/*.tex`, `figures/fig1_caption.tex`, `figures/fig_protocol.tex` (worktree I2).
- Framework: `/Users/palaash/Developer/deadlock-screen` at `0af7cb8` (README.md, docs/, CITATION.cff, .zenodo.json, and the public names in `deadlock_screen/`).

Paper paths below are relative to the I2 worktree and framework paths to the deadlock-screen root.

## Verdict

Most names agree exactly. These match with nothing to fix: the five classes and their one-word glosses (P prevent, L reshape, T heat, D steer, N glitch); every cell of Table 2; the probe set and its order; "screening protocol"; "rate-matched surrogate"; "snapshot", "frozen deadlock" and "snapshot library"; "lone trap" and "pair standoff"; SFM, SFM+TTC and SFM+ORCA (`model/params.py:36-39`); "legacy linear-program solver", "RVO2 solver" and "leg-normal sign defect"; "force coupling", "velocity coupling" and "rest drive", with its drive and constraint parts; time to free, handedness (lower/upper member) and ell. The framework also points to the right places in the paper: every Sect., Eq., Fig. and Table number it cites matches `paper/main.aux` (Eq. 6, 9, 10, 11; Sect. 3, 4.5, 5.2, 6, 6.1; Figs. 1-9; Tables 1-3). The framework's copies of Table 2, the Fig. 1 caption and the protocol figure match the paper apart from their comment lines. All 454 number macros that the paper uses have the same value in `reproduce/expected/numbers.tex`. The title, the authors, the ORCIDs, the affiliation and the earlier SIMULTECH paper (DOI, pages 474-481) agree with `paper/main.tex` and `refs.bib`.

The real mismatches are the deadlock-type names (group standoff vs `collective`), "preferred velocity" in the velocity-coupling equation, the double meaning of "perturbation", "resolver" vs "mechanism under test", the requirements listed in Sect. 8.3, and the description of what is archived on Zenodo.

## D. Experiment names

The paper never uses the framework's experiment identifiers (`lone_trap_library`, `quench_probes`, `activated_escape`, `timestep_signature`, `equal_temperature`, `pair_standoff`, `wall_law_sweep`, `contact_arches`, `rate_matched_runs`, `croma_comparison`, `velocity_coupling`). It refers to sections instead, so there is nothing to align. The framework's docstrings cite the paper's section and figure numbers correctly. One cosmetic point: the paper repository's figure files are named one number lower than the published figures (`figures/fig2_lone_trap.pdf` is Fig. 3, and so on up to `fig8_croma.pdf` = Fig. 9), while the framework's `reproduce/figures/fig3_lone_trap.py` ... `fig9_croma.py` follow the published numbering. This is invisible to readers.

Two further consistency points inside the paper, not involving the framework: "SFM + noise $\sigma^\ast$" (`07:5,12,20`; Table 3 caption) is written with spaces while "SFM+ORCA" is not; and "second trap" names both a snapshot class and the agent held after the blocker is removed (`05:18`, "The second trap is itself stuck alone"). Both are clear in context.
