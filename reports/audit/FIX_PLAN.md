## 0. How to use this plan

**Ownership.** Every item has exactly one *owner file*, and only the session that owns a work package (§4) edits its files. When an item needs a number that does not exist yet, the registry package (WP-REG) adds the macro under the name given here, and the section package uses that name. Cross-file items are split into one item per file, and each item names its partners.

**Order of the wave.**
1. **WP-0**: the glossary and wording tokens (§3), logged in `plan/CHANGELOG.md`. Every package applies them in its own files.
2. **WP-REG, WP-AN, WP-BIB, WP-REPO and WP-FIG** in parallel with the section packages. WP-REG and WP-AN publish macro names first; values may arrive later.
3. **WP-00 … WP-10, WP-T1/T2/T3** in parallel, one session per file.
4. **WP-RUN** after the experiment queue (E11, E8, E9, E6p) frees the machine, and only after Palaash approves (§6). Items marked *(after run-Nx)* get a provisional text now and their final text when the run lands.
5. The final page pass and `make paper` / `make submission`, then a re-audit of the changed lines.

**Effort scale:** XS < 10 min · S 10–30 min · M 30–90 min · L > 90 min. CPU-h is given for runs.

**Types:** prose · claim · number · structure · figure · compliance · new-run (an *analysis* of existing data is typed "claim" or "number" with "A:" in the fix).

### WP-00: `paper/sections/00_abstract.tex` (main.tex 651–654)

**00-1** · Sources PA F1.6, F9.1, F16.5, F16.6, W13.2; S1.1, S1.5, S1.10, S1.14; NC M10; CP L13 · Location 652 (`00_abstract.tex:3`) · Fix: replace the abstract with the text below (≈205 words, within 150–250). > When adding a steering paradigm to a pedestrian model clears a deadlock that the base model keeps, is the paradigm the reason? A control that shares none of the paradigm's machinery and is tuned to reproduce its rate shows that a simpler cause would suffice for that rate; whether the two act alike needs a separate test. We propose a calibrate-then-falsify protocol: each resolution claim is assigned to a physically defined mechanism class, and the matched control is confronted with the paradigm on signatures not used in the calibration, measured on a library of frozen deadlock states. In a social force model at a 0.8 m exit, the deadlocks of the last agents at the door are of two types: a lone agent held by the wall force at the door edges and, with a short-range jamb wall law, a pair standoff, a strict energy minimum that a lateral bias removes. Three controls reproduce the rate of optimal reciprocal collision avoidance (ORCA), added to the model as a force: noise matched on lone-trap freeing, noise matched on whole-run completion, and a lateral bias matched on pair freeing. Each differs from ORCA in replay, time-step dependence, time to completion or handedness. ORCA adds a goal drive, noise causes activated escape over barriers computed from the model's energy landscape, and the bias removes the pair minimum. Compared like for like with the CroMa bottleneck experiments, every model variant flows at 0.40–0.59 of the human rate.
