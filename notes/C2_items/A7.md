# A7 Temperature along the unstable mode, and the Arrhenius slope ratios

Script `experiments/revision2/A7_mode_temperature.py` writes `data/derived/rev2_A7.json`. The registry entries are in `notes/C2_items/A7.yaml` (prefix RvAseven). No dynamics was run, and the CPU time was 42 s, most of it the A4 bootstrap refits.

## What was done
- **Data.**
  - The E4 cells: rates, events, σ and the blocker's k_BT_eff (`E4_analysis.json`).
  - The unit unstable eigenvector of the saddle Hessian of U in the full condition (`fix_a3.json`, `unstable_mode_hessian`). The dynamic (Langer) unstable mode is used as a check.
  - The per-agent τ_i, read from the snapshots. They run from 0.41 to 0.60 s.
- **Rule.** Every agent receives the same per-step σ, so Eq. (10) (`experiments/lib/teff.py`, Δt = 0.01 s, m = 80 kg) gives each agent its own temperature, k_BT_eff(τ_i) = m σ² / (1 − (1 − Δt/τ_i)²). This is close to proportional to τ_i. The temperature along the unstable mode is taken as
  k_BT_mode = Σ_i p_i k_BT_eff(τ_i), with p_i = e_{i,x}² + e_{i,y}² and Σ p_i = 1.
  This is m times the variance of the projection of independent agent velocities on the unit unstable direction. All masses are 80 kg, so mass weighting does not change e.
- **Consequence.** Because σ is common to all agents, k_BT_mode = c k_BT_blocker, where c = Σ p_i k_BT_eff(τ_i) / k_BT_eff(τ_b) is the same at every level. On the abscissa of the E4 fit, 1/k_BT is replaced by (1/c)/k_BT_blocker. The ratio r = −slope/ΔU and its interval are therefore multiplied by c exactly; refitting from the counts reproduces c·r to 4e-16. The levels become β_mode = β/c.
- **Conditions.**
  - Full: c is computed as above.
  - Pinned ("frozen"): only the blocker is mobile. The neighbours are reset to their snapshot positions every step, so their noise does not enter, and c = 1.
  - Alone: c = 1.
  - B3 (alone, β up to 7): unchanged.
- **Recomputed with r_mode.** The per-condition and pooled medians, and the A4 regressions of r on ln(ΔU/J): OLS with 4000 snapshot-bootstrap draws (PCG64(20261110) as in A4), fitted for the full condition alone, pooled with one intercept per condition, and pooled with a common intercept. The blocker-temperature OLS slopes reproduce `rev_A4.json` exactly. The bootstrap draws come in a different order, so the blocker intervals differ from A4's in the third digit.

Per snapshot (full condition):
- In 6 of the 8 fitted snapshots the blocker carries 0.88–0.97 of the unstable mode, and c lies within 2% of 1.
- **E1b_seed61.** The blocker has the shortest τ of the E4 snapshots (0.41 s, weight 0.59). Here c = 1.16 and the ratio moves from 0.54 to 0.62 (interval [0.29, 0.94]). It still excludes 1.
- **E1b_seed158.** The blocker's weight is 0.02. The mode is carried by two neighbours with τ = 0.53 and 0.49 s against the blocker's 0.58 s. Here c = 0.91 and the ratio moves from 1.18 to 1.06.

## Interpretation
The noise heats each agent in proportion to its τ, so the agents of one snapshot are at temperatures that differ by up to 45% (τ 0.41–0.60 s). The escape is activated along the unstable mode of the saddle. In most full snapshots that mode belongs to the blocker: the neighbours make way, but they hardly move along the escape direction. The blocker's τ is then the right temperature to within 2%. Taking the temperature along the mode changes the ratio by more than 10% only where the mode is carried by agents other than the blocker (E1b_seed158) or where the blocker's τ is far from its neighbours' (E1b_seed61). Even there it does not change whether the interval covers 1.

The median full ratio falls from 1.2 to 1.1, and the pooled median stays at 0.95. The A4 regressions on ln ΔU are unchanged to two figures. The full-condition rise of the ratio with ΔU (b = 0.14) stays, with an interval that excludes 0.

The choice of temperature therefore does not explain B3's slope of 0.86–0.93 of the string barrier, nor the scatter of the full ratios. One caveat applies to the method: with unequal agent temperatures the stationary state is not a Boltzmann distribution, so k_BT_mode is an effective temperature for the escape direction, not an equilibrium temperature. Where the blocker dominates the mode the question does not arise.

## Paper claims
- No **CLAIM** changes. The E4 statement that each level sets ΔU/k_BT_eff "with the blocker's τ" is justified for the escape coordinate: the unstable mode is the blocker's in 6 of 8 fitted full snapshots, and in the pinned and alone conditions by construction.
- Add one sentence to Sect. 5.2 or its appendix stating that the result is insensitive to this choice.
