# B2 Time step of the width runs (dt = 0.005 s against 0.01 s)

Runs `experiments/revision2/B2_dt.py`: SFM (C1), CF flags, default walls, 100 agents, seeds 42-141, dt = 0.005 s,
widths 0.80, 0.88, 0.96 m, 600 s, deadlock stop (time-based: every active |v| < 0.01 m/s for 10 s, no exit);
runner `experiments/revision/B2_width.run`, unchanged. dt = 0.01 s: the same seeds and widths from E1b (0.80) and
session C's B2 (0.88, 0.96). Analysis `B2dt_analyze.py` -> `data/derived/rev2_B2.json`.

| width (m) | 0.80 | 0.88 | 0.96 |
|---|---|---|---|
| complete, dt = 0.005 s | 1 | 56 | 97 |
| complete, dt = 0.01 s | 2 | 52 | 96 |
| McNemar p | 1.0 | 0.42 | 1.0 |

Pooled over the 300 paired runs: \RvBtwoFineOnly runs complete only at 0.005 s and \RvBtwoCoarseOnly only at 0.01 s
(p = \RvBtwoPooledPDisp). Width at half completion from the three widths: \RvBtwoWfiftyFine m (0.005 s) against
\RvBtwoWfiftyCoarse m (0.01 s); shift \RvBtwoShift m [\RvBtwoShiftLo, \RvBtwoShiftHi] (paired bootstrap of seeds
within width, 2000 resamples).

Limits: three widths, one of them near the midpoint, and a two-parameter logistic per time step; the interval is
for a location shift under that shape, not a free curve. The eleven-width w50 (0.878 m, \RevBtwoWfifty) uses
dt = 0.01 s only.

Interpretation: halving the time step changes neither the completion at any width nor the width at half
completion beyond 0.009 m. The width transition is resolved at dt = 0.01 s (consistent with A12: overlaps below
1 cm at default speed). **No claim change.**
