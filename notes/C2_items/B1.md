# B1 Density-throttle ablation (veff_throttle = off)

Code: `code/sim/steering/hybrid.py`, flag `veff_throttle` in {on (default), off}; off passes no density to
`compute_desired_force`, so v_eff = v0 in the desired force (Eq. (2) without the Weidmann factor). The ORCA gate
w_o and the crush weight still read the local density (both inactive in these runs: crush off under CF, gate
w_o = 1, item A2 of session C). Default bit-identical (regression hash e381b49dc24acccd OK;
`code/tests/test_veff_throttle.py`). Runs `experiments/revision2/B1_throttle.py`, analysis `B1_analyze.py` ->
`data/derived/rev2_B1.json`.

## (a) Width sweep, SFM, throttle off

SFM (C1), CF flags, default walls, 100 agents, seeds 42-141 (the spawns of the width runs), widths 0.80, 0.84, 0.88,
0.92, 0.96 m, 600 s, deadlock stop; 500 runs. Throttle on: the same seeds and widths from E1b (0.80) and session C's
B2 (0.84-0.96). Logistic MLE, w50 with the bootstrap interval of `experiments/revision/B2_analyze.w50_fit`.

| width (m) | 0.80 | 0.84 | 0.88 | 0.92 | 0.96 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| complete, off | 0 | 20 | 53 | 86 | 96 |
| complete, on  | 2 | 15 | 52 | 82 | 96 |

- w50 off = \RvBoneWfiftyOff [\RvBoneWfiftyOffLo, \RvBoneWfiftyOffHi] m (0.878 [0.872, 0.884]); on, same five widths:
  \RvBoneWfiftyOnSame (0.881 [0.875, 0.887]); published eleven-width w50 0.878 [0.874, 0.883] (\RevBtwoWfifty).
  Shift from the published value \RvBoneDwfiftyPub m (below 10^-3 m), from the same-width fit \RvBoneDwfiftySame m. **No HEADLINE CHANGE**: w50 off lies inside the published interval.
- Paired by seed over all 500 runs: \RvBoneWidthOffOnly runs complete only without the throttle, \RvBoneWidthOnOnly
  only with it (McNemar p = \RvBoneWidthPDisp).

Interpretation. At the door the throttle is nearly inactive for the runs that decide completion: the deadlock is a
lone or pair trap at rest whose holding force is m v_eff/τ against the jamb walls, and the local density over a 3 m
disc at the end of a run (a few agents left) gives a Weidmann factor close to 1. Earlier in the run the throttle
slows the crowd, which reorders arrivals but does not change how often the last agents are stuck.

## (b) w = 0.8 m whole runs

w = 0.8 m, 100 agents, seeds 242-341 (spawns of E6a and the velocity-coupling whole runs), 600 s, `runlib.whole_run`;
throttle on: velorca "sfm" and E6a at the same seeds. Paired by seed.

| | throttle off | throttle on | discordant off-only / on-only | McNemar p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SFM | 1/100 (\RvBoneSfmOffK) | 1/100 | 1 / 1 | 1.0 |
| force-coupled ORCA (RVO2) | 53/100 [0.43, 0.62] (\RvBoneOrcaOffK) | 54/100 | 17 / 18 | \RvBoneOrcaPDisp |

Force-coupled ORCA without the throttle: 13 deadlocks and 34 runs still moving at 600 s
(\RvBoneOrcaOffDeadlock, \RvBoneOrcaOffCapped). Completion does not change; which spawns complete does
(35 discordant of 100), as expected of a chaotic many-body run whose early dynamics the throttle alters.
**No HEADLINE CHANGE**: the SFM/ORCA completion gap at 0.8 m holds without the throttle.
