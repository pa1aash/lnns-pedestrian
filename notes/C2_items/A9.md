# A9: CroMa comparison (Sect. 7): length, motivation, 10%–90% flow, headline ratio, data-paper facts

Script `experiments/revision2/A9_croma_compare.py` (reads existing data only, about 15 CPU-s). Output
`data/derived/rev2_A9.json`. Registry `notes/C2_items/A9.yaml` (170 macros, prefix RvAnine; build_registry2.py green).

## Data and rules

- Model: the 100 D3 runs (`data/revision/D3/d3_croma.jsonl`). There are 4 variants (C1_cf, C1_cf_edge, C3_rvo2_cf,
  C1_cf_sig) × 5 model runs per human run, and the agent number equals the participants who passed (135, 135, 129,
  129, 128). Raw trajectories come from the D3 raw directory (the session-C copy; this worktree has none).
- Humans: `data/ext/croma/*_w080_*.txt`, participant rule of D2 (duplicate track IDs dropped).
- Passage: the first frame at or past the entrance plane of a track that starts before it. Run flow = (n − 1)/(last − first passage),
  where n is the number of passages observed. This is the E10/D3 estimator.
- Ratio of arithmetic means model/human. The 95% interval comes from the **delta method on the log ratio with Welch df**
  (fix_a7.ratio_means, the headline estimator). The secondary interval is a percentile bootstrap that resamples model
  and human runs independently within the group (20 000 draws, seed 2026). With 2 or 3 human runs the bootstrap
  understates the human spread, so it serves only as a comparison. Per human run, the ratio is the mean of its 5 model
  runs over the human value, with a t interval over those 5 model runs.
- 10%–90% passage flow over N agents: k10 = ⌈0.1N⌉ − 1 and k90 = ⌈0.9N⌉ − 1 (0-based ranks), and the flow is
  (k90 − k10)/(t[k90] − t[k10]). **Censoring rule.** A run with fewer than k90 + 1 passages is censored. Rule UB sets its
  value to (k90 − k10)/(600 s − t[k10]), an upper bound. Rule LB sets it to 0. The two rules bracket the ratio.
  **No run is censored** (RvAnineWindowCensTotal = 0). Every deadlocked or capped run leaves at most a few agents behind,
  well under 10%, so UB and LB coincide. The window flows of all 49 incomplete runs (23 + 4 + 15 + 7) are real values.
  Human window flows under this N rule are 1.49 pooled. D2's rule over the observed passages (round) gave 1.48.

## (iii) Headline ratio: definition and recomputation

**Definition** (D3_analyze.py → D_croma_integrity.d4 → fix_a7.ratio_means). For each variant the ratio is the arithmetic
mean of the run flows over its 25 runs, divided by the arithmetic mean of the 5 corrected human flows. Deadlocked and
capped runs count with the passages they made, so the flow is counted up to the deadlock or the 600 s horizon. The
model runs are pooled over both lengths and all three motivations. RevDThreeRatioMin–Max is the smallest and largest of
the four point ratios.

Recomputed under this definition, the range is **RvAnineRatioMin–RvAnineRatioMax = 0.40–0.60**. That is 0.4008 (noise)
to 0.5958 (force-coupled ORCA), identical to D3 to machine precision (`headline.reproduces_D3: true`). **It does not change.**

## (iv) Motivation conditions (data paper Sect. 3.7, p. 20; Table 19, App. A.9)

The levels are h0 normal, h1 hurry and h2 full commitment, set by a spoken instruction before each run.
4D030 h0, 4D040 h1, 4D110 h0, 4D120 h1, 4D130 h2. That is **2 normal, 2 hurry, 1 full commitment**
(RvAnineMotivNormalN, RvAnineMotivHurryN, RvAnineMotivFullN). 4D130 also differs in line-up: the crowd stood
directly at the bottleneck rather than behind a 2 m semicircle, and participants could sit it out (hdf5 note, D).
The two 2.0 m runs are h0 and h1. The 0.2 m runs are h0, h1 and h2.

## (v) Body size: **shoulder width 0.45 m (SD 0.04 m)**

Boomers et al., arXiv:2303.02319v2, **p. 7, Sect. 2.4**: the mean shoulder width of the participants was 45 cm (SD 4 cm).
That figure covers all 1038 participants of the four days, measured at registration. Day 4 appears only as a histogram
(App. A.1, Fig. 19, no number). Macros RvAnineShoulderWidth = 0.45 and RvAnineShoulderWidthSD = 0.04. Half the shoulder
width is RvAnineShoulderHalf = 0.225 m, so **A1's placeholder 0.225 m radius is the documented value**.
The model draws r ~ N(0.25, 0.03) m, floored at 0.15 m (code/sim/core/agent.py). Its mean diameter is
RvAnineModelDiameter = 0.50 m (SD 0.06). That exceeds the mean shoulder width by RvAnineDiameterExcess = 0.05 m, a factor
of RvAnineDiameterOverShoulder = 1.1, or RvAnineDiameterExcessSD = 1.25 human SDs. The model disc is therefore wider
than the average participant's shoulders. That works against the model flow at a 0.8 m entrance. This item does not
quantify the effect, so it is no correction to the ratio.

## (vi) Ethics statement (data paper, p. 34, "Ethical Review")

The bottleneck experiment was submitted by A. Seyfried to the ethical review committee of the University of Wuppertal
(Bergische Universität Wuppertal). It "was approved in January 2020 (file reference MS/BBL 191213 Seyfried)". The other
CroMa experiments were approved by the German Psychological Society (DGPs) in December 2019 under a separate reference.
On consent, p. 5, Sect. 2.3: at registration participants signed that they agreed to the conditions of participation.
They also filled in a declaration of informed consent, which was checked before they took part.
The journal version (Collective Dynamics 8, A141) could not be downloaded here. If the paper cites the journal version,
its page numbers need checking.

## Claims

- **Not a CLAIM change:** the range 0.40–0.60 stands under its definition. The text should state that definition.
  The ratio is the mean model run flow, counted from first to last passage up to any deadlock or the 600 s horizon,
  over the mean human flow.
- **CLAIM (framing):** "every variant passes 0.40–0.60 of the human flow" pools human runs of three motivations. Against
  the two normal-motivation runs the variants reach RvAnineCSigNormalRatio–RvAnineCThreeNormalRatio = 0.45–0.68. Against
  full commitment (4D130) they reach 0.33–0.53. Since the model has no motivation, the ratio depends on which human
  condition is the reference, and the text should say that the five runs are 2 normal, 2 hurry and 1 full commitment.
- **CLAIM (Sect. 7, window flow):** the 10%–90% comparison applies to all variants. It gives 0.54–0.64 with no
  censored run, not only the SFM number now quoted. In that measure the noise variant is not the slowest.
- The length split cannot be resolved with two 2.0 m human runs. "With the 2.0 m bottleneck ..." should stay
  descriptive.
