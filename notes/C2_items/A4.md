# A4 β span of the time-step probe (E3)

Script `experiments/revision2/A4_e3_beta.py` -> `data/derived/rev2_A4.json`; registry `notes/C2_items/A4.yaml`
(prefix RvAfour). CPU time 3 s. No simulations.

## Data, rule, method
- E3 (`experiments/E3/e3_dt.py`, `data/E3/e3_dt.jsonl`, `data/derived/E3_analysis.json`): 20 lone-trap snapshots
  (gate-accepted, full ΔU ≥ 3 J, ranks round(linspace(0, 35, 20)) by ΔU), ΔU = 3.9-57 J, blocker τ_b 0.40-0.60 s.
- Noise level (one per snapshot): σ_i = sqrt(k_BT_target (1 - (1 - 0.01/τ_b)^2)/m) with k_BT_target = ΔU_i/β_ref,
  β_ref = 1.5 for even ΔU rank and 2.5 for odd rank (10 snapshots each). So β = β_ref at Δt = 0.01 s in both arms.
- Arms: per-step (fixed σ_i at every Δt; the arm whose log rate ratio ℓ is the probe, Eq. ell) and √Δt-scaled
  (σ_i sqrt(Δt/0.01 s), k_BT_eff about fixed; the control).
- β = ΔU_i / k_BT_eff(σ, Δt, τ_b) with Eq. (teff) (`experiments/lib/teff.py`) at Δt = 0.005, 0.01, 0.02 s;
  recomputed values equal the stored per-row and per-cell β exactly (maximum deviation 0).

## Results

| arm | β_ref (at Δt = 0.01 s) | Δt = 0.005 s | Δt = 0.01 s | Δt = 0.02 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| per-step | 1.5 (10 snapshots) | 0.75 (RvAfourLowFine; 0.753-0.754) | 1.5 | 2.97-2.97 (RvAfourLowCoarse = 3.0) |
| per-step | 2.5 (10 snapshots) | 1.26 (RvAfourHighFine = 1.3; 1.255-1.258) | 2.5 | 4.94-4.96 (RvAfourHighCoarse = 5.0) |
| √Δt-scaled | 1.5 | 1.51 | 1.5 | 1.48 |
| √Δt-scaled | 2.5 | 2.51 | 2.5 | 2.47 |

Overall span: per-step β = 0.75-5.0 (RvAfourPerStepMin, RvAfourPerStepMax); √Δt-scaled β = 1.5-2.5
(RvAfourScaledMin, RvAfourScaledMax; 1.48-2.52). The per-step β at Δt = 0.005 and 0.02 s is 0.50 and 1.98 times the
Δt = 0.01 s value (RvAfourRatioFine, RvAfourRatioCoarse) for every snapshot; the per-snapshot spread comes from τ_b
only (factor (1 - (1 - Δt/τ)^2)/(1 - (1 - 0.01/τ)^2)). 20 of the 60 per-step cells (RvAfourCellsBelowOneHalf of
RvAfourCellsPerArm, all at Δt = 0.005 s) lie below β = 1.5, 10 below β = 1 (RvAfourCellsBelowOne, the β_ref = 1.5
snapshots at Δt = 0.005 s). The per-snapshot table is `rev2_A4.json:per_snapshot`.

## Interpretation
At fixed per-step σ, k_BT_eff ∝ 1/Δt to leading order, so halving and doubling Δt from 0.01 s halves and doubles
β; the probe tests the Arrhenius law over a factor 4 in β per snapshot, from 0.75-1.3 at the finest step to
3.0-5.0 at the coarsest. The √Δt-scaled control keeps β within 1.5 % of β_ref. The observed ℓ agrees with the
prediction at β_ref = 1.5 (mean observed / predicted 0.98, E3_analysis.json ell.per_step.by_level) although its
fine-step end lies at β = 0.75, and falls short at β_ref = 2.5 (0.87), where the span is 1.3-5.0.

**CLAIM (wording).** The revision vocabulary reserves "activated escape" for β ≥ 1.5 with the blocker alone. The time-step
probe's per-step arm spans β = 0.75-5.0, so its Δt = 0.005 s end (all 20 snapshots, β = 0.75-1.3) is below that
range; the probe's statement "the escape rate under per-step noise follows Eq. (ell)" must name the β span and
should not be read as a test of the Kramers regime at Δt = 0.005 s. E3 also uses the full (all residual agents
mobile) barrier, not the blocker alone.
