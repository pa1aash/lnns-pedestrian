## Interpretation

The completion headline reproduces under this model with either flag set. Force-coupled ORCA completes about half of
the runs at w = 0.8 m (58% legacy, 54% RVO2, against the conference's 52%), and the SFM completes 1.5-1.7%. The gap
is 53-56 percentage points, as in the conference (54). The SFM's completion is small and nonzero: the lone wall-law
trap holds all but 3 of 200 (published) and 5 of 300 (CF) runs. The conference's 0/25 is the seeds 42-66 realisation of that process
(0/25 here too with published flags), so "categorical" holds only to within an upper bound of 4.3% (published) or
3.8% (CF). The noise control at sigma = 0.05 m/s completes 57% and 61% against 13/25 (52%).

The mechanism attribution is what changes (Sect. 6, A6 of the corrections table); the completion numbers that
carried it stand. C2 and C4 are expected to follow from the rest-state lemma: TTC exerts no force at rest, so C2
should complete like C1 and C4 like C3; the queued runs test this seed by seed.

**CLAIM** (Table 4 / corrections row A3, "pending (E6)"): C3 is now confirmed for both solvers (29/50 legacy,
101/186 RVO2, each consistent with 13/25). Row A1 stays revised (the SFM's 0/25 is a small-n zero; 3/200 with
published flags). C4 remains unconfirmed until A11 runs; with published flags it is not affordable here (21 CPU-h
per 100).
