# A5 Equivalence of the freed fraction (TOST, margin ±0.10) and the window of horizons

Script `experiments/revision2/A5_equivalence.py` -> `data/derived/rev2_A5.json`; registry `notes/C2_items/A5.yaml`
(prefix `RvAfive`). Existing data only, 4 s CPU, one process.

## What was done

- **Data.** The loaders of `experiments/revision/A7_paired.py` and `A8_horizon.py`, with the exit times kept so that
  the outcome can be read at any horizon T:
  - lone keystones (34) and second traps (26): `data/E2/e2_quench.jsonl`, blocker exit time ≤ T;
  - σ = 0.0717 m/s, 20 seeds: `data/N1/n1.jsonl`;
  - the 134 second traps E2 did not draw: `data/revision/A5/a5_quench.jsonl` (A5x), pooled with E2's 26 to 160;
  - whole runs, seeds 242–427 (186): `data/E6/e6a.jsonl` (ORCA RVO2), `e6b.jsonl` (SFM + σ* = 0.0467 m/s);
    completed and evacuation time ≤ T;
  - pair standoffs (46): `data/E7b/e7b_quench.jsonl`, `data/E7b_bstar/bstar_quench.jsonl` (arms `bstar_pub+`,
    `bstar_rvo2+`); freed and first member exit ≤ T.
- **Difference.** Per unit i (snapshot; spawn seed for whole runs), d_i(T) = p_i(T) − c_i(T): p_i is the surrogate's
  freed fraction over its seeds (the unperturbed run for the bias), c_i ∈ {0, 1} is ORCA's unperturbed outcome.
  Δ(T) = mean d_i, surrogate minus ORCA.
- **Intervals.** Primary: cluster bootstrap over units (a snapshot is resampled with all its seeds), 10 000
  percentile resamples, a fresh `Generator(PCG64(12345))` per comparison, the same resample matrix at every T.
  Secondary: the paired t interval on the d_i (df = n − 1), with the TOST p (the larger of the two one-sided p).
- **Equivalence.** Margin ±0.10. TOST at 5%: the 90% interval lies inside (−0.10, +0.10). The 95% interval inside the
  margin is reported beside. A bound equal to the margin counts as outside.
- **Margin.** 0.10 is the 95% binomial half-width of ORCA's own freed fraction on the 34–46-snapshot samples
  (Wilson half-widths 0.10–0.14 there; \RvAfiveHalfwidthMin–\RvAfiveHalfwidthMax over all samples), so a tighter
  margin would demand more precision than the reference measurement has; it equals 3–5 snapshots of 34–46.
- **Window.** Δ(T) on T = 1, 2, …, 120 s (snapshots, pairs) and 5, 10, …, 600 s (whole runs). The window is the set
  of T at which the 90% interval lies inside the margin; the interval of T that contains the nominal horizon is
  named. T at which both arms free fewer than 10% of the units is marked degenerate (nothing is freed yet).

## Interpretation

- **Lone keystone.** Noise at the matched level frees the same fraction of blockers as force-coupled ORCA to within
  ±0.10 once T ≥ 86–89 s. ORCA frees its 30 blockers within 7 s and is flat afterwards; the noise freed fraction
  rises with T as further thermally assisted escapes accumulate, and enters the margin late in the window. At 60 s
  the match fails (A8: Δ = −0.09 [−0.16, −0.01]). The 20-seed level σ = 0.0717 is equivalent at both 90% and 95%.
- **Second trap.** Noise frees 13 percentage points more blockers than ORCA (RVO2) on 160 snapshots, and the 90%
  interval reaches 0.18, beyond the +0.10 edge: the noise is not equivalent at 120 s, and the excess is positive. Against RVO2 it is
  equivalent only between 22 and 43 s, the interval of T in which the noise has caught up with ORCA's early exits
  (ORCA frees its last second-trap blocker at 36 s) and has not yet overtaken it. Against the legacy solver, which
  frees more second-trap blockers (133/160), the same noise is equivalent at 90% from 23 s on.
- **Whole runs.** σ* is equivalent to ORCA (RVO2) in completed fraction at 600 s, and only from 545 s on. ORCA
  completes all its 101 runs by 161 s; noise completions keep arriving until the cap, so the window is the last
  minute of the 600 s horizon.
- **Pair bias.** The bias arm frees one standoff more than ORCA with either solver, but 17 (legacy) and 15 (RVO2) of
  the 46 standoffs are discordant (A7: 9:8 and 8:7), so the paired difference has a 90% interval of ±0.15. The
  sample of 46 deterministic pairs cannot establish equivalence at ±0.10; a margin of about ±0.17 would be needed.
  The match holds as "not separated", and b* frees the target count by construction (A6 tests that out of sample).

## Claims

- **CLAIM** (Sect. 6.1, Table "matched noise"). "Fraction-matched at T = 120 s" for σ = 0.07 and σ = 0.0717 holds as
  equivalence within ±0.10 (TOST p = 0.032 and 0.0048), and only for T ≥ 89 s and 86 s respectively.
- **CLAIM** (second trap). Noise σ = 0.07 is shown non-equivalent to ORCA (RVO2) at 120 s: the 90% interval
  [0.085, 0.18] on 160 snapshots lies above the margin's lower edge and crosses its upper edge, TOST p = 0.86. The
  match exists only for 22 s ≤ T ≤ 43 s. This strengthens A6/A5x of round C (interval excluded 0) to a statement
  about the size of the gap.
- **CLAIM** (Sect. 6.2). σ* is equivalent to ORCA (RVO2) in completed fraction at 600 s (TOST p = 0.026), window
  545–600 s.
- **CLAIM** (Sect. 6.3, Table "matched bias"). The bias b* is not shown equivalent to ORCA in freed fraction at
  ±0.10 with either solver (TOST p = 0.20, 0.18); the text should say "not separated" (95% interval covers 0) and
  not "matched" in the equivalence sense, and quote the interval.
