# A12 Largest body overlaps at default speed (room) and in the CroMa runs

Script `experiments/revision2/A12_overlap.py` (`run` writes `data/revision2/A12/a12.jsonl`, `analyse` writes
`data/derived/rev2_A12.json`); registry `notes/C2_items/A12.yaml` (prefix RvAtwelve). 20 reruns in one process,
CPU 0.69 h (RvAtwelveCPUh; room 0.16 h, CroMa SFM 0.12 h, CroMa force-coupled ORCA 0.41 h).

## Data, rule, method
- No stored run (E1b, E7, B2, E10, D3) logs overlaps or full positions at every step (E10/D3 keep 25 Hz tracks of
  agents with x > 6.5 m only, and the raw files are not in this worktree), so a sample is rerun with a per-step
  recorder. The recorder only reads the state; every rerun reproduces its stored record (t_end, completion,
  deadlock identical for all 20 runs).
- Room: C1 with the CF flags (the E1b mode), w = 0.8 m, 100 agents, default desired speeds (mean 1.34 m/s), seeds
  42-51, `w2lib.build`, Δt = 0.01 s, 600 s cap, stationary-deadlock stop (as `experiments/E1/e1_library.py`).
  All 10 runs end in a deadlock.
- CroMa: D3 cells C1_cf (SFM) and C3_rvo2_cf (force-coupled ORCA, RVO2), seeds 42-46, one run of each human run
  type 4D030-4D130 (passed-participant N = 128-135, L = 2.0 / 0.2 m), jobs of `D3_croma_rerun.py`, scenario and stop
  rule of `E10/e10_croma.py run()`.
- After every step, over active agents: agent-agent overlap max_{i<j}(r_i + r_j - d_ij) and wall overlap
  max_i(r_i - min_W d_iW) (segment distance minus the wall's capsule radius, 0 for sharp walls). Negative values are
  the smallest gap (no contact). Per run the maximum over all steps; also the maximum over t ≥ 1 s (spawn left out).
  Summary over runs: max, 99th percentile (numpy linear) and median of the per-run maxima.

No step in any run has an agent-agent overlap above 5 cm; 0 room steps, 15 CroMa-SFM steps and 10 CroMa-ORCA steps
exceed 1 cm (of 154 200, 97 593 and 165 451). The median over steps of the per-step maximum is a gap of 8-9.5 cm in
every set. Largest single overlap: room seed 49 at t = 17.1 s, two agents (r = 0.20, 0.27 m) side by side at the door
(x = 9.7 m), 1.3% of the radius sum; CroMa SFM seed 46 at t = 1.1 s in front of the entrance (5.0% of the radius sum);
CroMa ORCA seed 42 at t = 0.15 s in the waiting area during the start (late maximum 1.5 cm).
The 99th percentile of 5 or 10 run maxima lies between the two largest values, so it is close to the maximum.

## Interpretation
At default desired speeds the social repulsion A exp((r_ij - d_ij)/B) keeps the agents apart: in 8 of 10 room runs
no two agents ever touch, and the largest overlap is 6 mm, against the 0.24 m single overlap of the 5 m/s arch runs
at the same time step (RevAthirteen). The body compression k(r_ij - d_ij) and the sliding friction of Eq. (sfm) are
therefore inactive in 8 of 10 default-speed room runs and act on sub-centimetre overlaps in the other two: the
lone trap is set by the exponential social and wall terms (the B_w = 0.04 m pair-standoff library was not rerun). The CroMa runs are denser (128-135 agents, a waiting crowd) and touch in 9 of 10
runs, with overlaps of at most 2.4 cm (5% of a radius sum) and wall overlaps up to 2.0 cm; Δt = 0.01 s resolves
these contacts (B4: under-resolution appears at overlaps of order 0.1 m).

**CLAIM (supports, scope).** "Agents spawn without overlap" can be extended: at default speeds bodies overlap by at
most 0.62 cm in the room and 2.4 cm in CroMa at Δt = 0.01 s, so the Δt = 0.01 s under-resolution found for the 5 m/s
arch runs (B4) does not reach the default-speed results. No existing claim changes.
