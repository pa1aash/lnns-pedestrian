Scripts experiments/revision4/T4_croma_dt.py (copy of the E10 run with a dt override, trajectory stride
round(0.04/dt), and a per-step contact-overlap recorder), T4_analyze.py → data/derived/rev4_T4.json; raw
data/revision4/T4/. Estimators of the published CroMa numbers unchanged (D3_analyze.load_run, D_croma_integrity.d4,
fix_a7.ratio_means: Welch log-ratio interval; Student t for means).

Cost rule: 25 runs per variant at both time steps ≈ 17 CPU-h > 12, so 10 runs per variant (seeds 42–51, two per
human run), at dt = 0.005 s and the same 10 seeds at dt = 0.01 s. The dt = 0.01 s records reproduce
data/revision/D3/d3_croma.jsonl exactly (40/40: end time, completion, passages, flow).

Friction factor at the largest agent overlap h, κ = 2.4e5 kg/(m s), m = 80 kg; the explicit tangential-friction
update is stable for a factor below 2. No step has an overlap above 5 cm in any run.

Checks:
- Every dt = 0.005 s flow ratio lies inside the dt = 0.01 s interval on the same 10 seeds and inside the published
  25-run interval (SFM [0.42, 0.59]; jamb range [0.47, 0.67]; force-coupled ORCA [0.50, 0.71]; noise [0.33, 0.48]).
- Paired by seed, flow(0.005)/flow(0.01), geometric mean [95%]: SFM 1.01 [0.96, 1.06]; jamb range 0.98 [0.93, 1.04];
  ORCA 0.97 [0.93, 1.02]; noise 1.06 [0.75, 1.49]. Completion discordance (0.005 only : 0.01 only): 1:1, 2:0, 1:2,
  3:1; McNemar p ≥ 0.50.

Physics: halving dt leaves flow and completion unchanged within the 10-run resolution for all four variants and the
fluctuation unchanged for the three noise-free ones. Under per-step noise the fluctuation rises from 0.108 to 0.124
m/s (intervals disjoint), as expected from k_B T_eff ∝ σ²/(1 − (1 − dt/τ)²) at fixed per-step σ. The largest overlaps
change by at most 0.4 cm (agents) and 0.6 cm (walls). With per-step noise at dt = 0.01 s the friction factor at the
largest overlap (3.75 cm, t = 1.17 s) is 2.25, above the explicit stability bound of 2; at dt = 0.005 s it is 1.00.
The SFM overlap at dt = 0.01 s (2.44 cm, 10 runs) agrees with the published 2.4 cm (A12, 5 runs), and force-coupled
ORCA (1.89 cm) with 1.9 cm.
