# T3 — Time-step probe at the matched noise σ = 0.07 m/s (20 E3 snapshots)

Scripts experiments/revision4/T3_dt_matched.py (2400 restarts), T3_analyze.py → data/derived/rev4_T3.json.
Protocol as E3 (alone condition at each E3 snapshot, C1 + CF, runlib.escape), except σ = 0.07 m/s for every
snapshot instead of E3's per-snapshot σ_i. 20 restarts per (snapshot, arm, dt); dt ∈ {0.005, 0.01, 0.02} s;
censoring 300 s (900 s for per-step at dt = 0.02 s, where β is highest). Seeds 51000 + 1000k + 10·round(1000 dt) + rep
(+500 for the scaled arm). Arms: per-step (σ per step fixed) and √dt-scaled (σ·√(dt/0.01)). At dt = 0.01 s the two
arms are the same run definition; both were run, with disjoint seeds.

Physics: with one σ for all snapshots, the per-step rate still rises as dt falls (ℓ > 0, interval excludes 0) because
k_B T_eff ∝ σ²/(1 − (1 − dt/τ)²) roughly doubles when dt halves; scaling σ by √dt holds T_eff, and hence β, fixed to
within 1.5%, and ℓ is consistent with 0. At matched σ, 15 of 20 snapshots have β < 1 at dt = 0.005 s under per-step
noise. The observed per-step ℓ lies 0.40 below the prediction with slope ≈ 1 against it: a constant offset, not a
different dependence on dU.
