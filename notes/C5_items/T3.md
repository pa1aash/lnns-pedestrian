# T3 — which rate theory gives the 0.90 slope ratio (alone condition, Arrhenius set)

Script experiments/revision7/T3_rate_theory.py → data/derived/rev7_T3.json. No runs: theory evaluated on the alone
potential of the 10 Arrhenius-set snapshots (E4), with the functions of experiments/revision2/A3_arrhenius.py
(theory) unchanged. Registry prefix RzTthree (notes/C5_items/revision7.yaml).

## Method
- β = ΔU/k_BT_eff, ΔU = saddle − minimum of the alone potential (bar.Field, ρ = 0); the alone span equals the saddle
  height on every barrier-set path (rev5_X1: 0 alone paths affected), so no span correction applies.
- (i) k_TST: exact 2-D transition-state rate, k_TST = sqrt(k_BT/2πm) Z_line/Z_well on a 4 mm grid of U (well x < x_saddle;
  anharmonic well and saddle line included). (ii) κ k_TST with Kramers' κ = sqrt(1 + (γ/2ω_b)²) − γ/2ω_b, γ = 1/τ,
  ω_b from the axial saddle curvature. (iii) A κ k_TST with Mel'nikov–Meshkov A(Δ), Δ = γ S(E_b)/k_BT.
- Slope ratio r = −d ln k / dβ by least squares over β = 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 5, 7 per snapshot; pooled with snapshot
  intercepts (unweighted). Prefactor ν(β) = k e^β; its temperature dependence is reported as ν(1.5)/ν(7) (T falls by
  4.7×) and the local exponent d ln ν / d ln T at both ends (finite differences on a 0.25 grid in β).

## Answer, with sign and size of each prefactor's temperature dependence
1. **Exact TST (anharmonic configurational integral) gives most of the shortfall: r = 0.92.** Its prefactor
   *increases* as T falls (ν_TST(1.5)/ν_TST(7) = 0.47–0.92, i.e. ν_TST
   rises 1.1–2.1× from β 1.5 to 7; d ln ν_TST/d ln T = −0.07 to −0.54 at β 1.5 and −0.03 to −0.44 at β 7, largest for the high barriers; −0.43 to −0.90 at β 1.5 with A included). Cause: at high T the
   well and saddle line are sampled beyond their harmonic region (the stiff wall makes the well anharmonic), so the
   effective attempt frequency is lower than the harmonic limit it approaches as T → 0. A rate whose prefactor grows as
   T falls has an apparent activation energy below ΔU, hence r < 1.
2. **κ is temperature independent** (constant γ and ω_b), so κ k_TST has exactly the k_TST ratio.
3. **A(Δ) supplies the rest: r falls to 0.89.** A rises from 0.80–0.86 at β 1.5 to 1.000 at β 7 (energy-diffusion
   depopulation matters only at high T, where Δ = γS/k_BT is smallest), again a prefactor that grows as T falls; it
   lowers r by 0.02–0.03 on every snapshot. Total ν_full(1.5)/ν_full(7) = 0.39–0.75.
4. Harmonic TST (Vineyard) or Kramers–Langer with constant prefactor give r = 1 exactly; they cannot produce 0.90.
   The 0.90 is the anharmonic TST prefactor (≈ 0.08 of the 0.10 shortfall, pooled) plus the Mel'nikov–Meshkov
   factor (≈ 0.03), not damping (κ contributes 0).
5. Size grows with ΔU: r_TST = 0.96–0.99 for ΔU < 17 J (seeds 60, 61, 72), 0.87–0.89 for ΔU > 27 J. At fixed β the
   high-barrier wells are probed over a larger range of x, where the wall potential departs most from quadratic.
