# T5 — split-sample matching of per-step noise to force-coupled ORCA (RVO2)

Script experiments/revision7/T5_split.py → data/derived/rev7_T5.json. No runs (existing E2, N1, E5b, E6 records).
Same split design as the bias test of rev2_A6: 200 random halves, numpy Generator(PCG64(2026)), first half fits,
second half is held out. Registry prefix RzFive.

## (1) Lone keystones (34; σ matched to ORCA's freed fraction within 120 s)
- Data: per-restart blocker outcome at σ = 0.03, 0.05, 0.07 m/s (E2, 5 seeds per snapshot) and 0.0717 (N1, 20 seeds);
  ORCA = unperturbed C3_rvo2 quench, 30/34 freed.
- Primary rule = the rule that set σ = 0.0717 (experiments/runs3/N1.py; fact 6e): binomial-logit fit over the three E2
  levels, inverted at ORCA's freed fraction. On all 34 it reproduces a = -2.0532, b = 56.70,
  σ = 0.0717 (check passed). Per split: fit on 17 → σ_S; held-out difference = the logit of the held-out
  17 evaluated at σ_S minus ORCA's freed fraction on them.
- **Held-out difference: median -0.006 [-0.182, +0.156]** (mean |e| 0.070);
  σ_S median 0.0712 [0.0597, 0.0917] m/s.
- Reference: the split alone (ORCA_H − ORCA_S) spreads +0.000 [-0.235, +0.235]; the held-out error is no larger than
  what any fit matched on half the snapshots must miss.
- Secondary (piecewise-linear interpolation of the observed freed fractions incl. N1's 0.0717): median
  -0.024 [-0.164, +0.041]; biased low because the observed curve flattens at 0.86–0.87 below ORCA's 0.88
  (106/200 fits hit the grid maximum 0.0717).

## (2) Whole runs (186 seeds 242–427; σ matched to ORCA's completion)
- Completion against σ exists only on E5b's seeds 42–141 (σ = 0.03–0.07, 100 runs each; the logit E6 used:
  a = -3.175, b = 71.74); on the 186 seeds noise was run only at σ* = 0.0467 (E6b, 99/186;
  ORCA 101/186). Fit on 93: σ_S = logit inversion at ORCA's completion on S. Held out: E6b's completion on H at σ*
  moved along the E5b logit slope to σ_S, minus ORCA's completion on H.
- **Held-out difference: median -0.006 [-0.125, +0.124]**; σ_S median 0.0464
  [0.0434, 0.0507]. Without the slope transfer (σ* on both halves): -0.011 [-0.086, +0.065].
  Split alone: +0.011 [-0.140, +0.118].
- Caveat: the slope comes from other seeds (42–141) and is not refitted per half; no σ other than σ* was run on 242–427.

## Comparison with the bias b* (rev2_A6, pair standoffs, 23/23 halves)

runs predicts the held-out half as well as the halves agree with each other (95% ranges ≈ ±0.17 and ±0.12, against
