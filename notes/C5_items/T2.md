# Task 2: alone-condition Arrhenius slope on 56 snapshots

Scripts experiments/revision7/T2_alone.py (exact batched alone integrator), T2_analyze.py → data/derived/rev7_T2.json. Raw data/revision7/T2/t2_alone.jsonl.

Design: 56 snapshots (10 E4, 46 new; 3 of them are the B3 set), β ∈ {1.5, …, 7}, 100 restarts per snapshot pooled over β; censored-exponential (Poisson) likelihood with snapshot intercepts; 1000 bootstrap resamples over snapshots (PCG64(2026)). Langer prefactor with the unstable eigenvalue (as fix_a3).

**Result.** Pooled slope ratio (measured / barrier) **0.946 [0.935, 0.961]**, 5088 events in 5600 restarts (512 censored).

| set | ratio [95%] |
|---|---|
| all 56 | 0.946 [0.935, 0.961] |
| without B3 (53) | 0.950 [0.937, 0.966] |
| E4 ten | 0.915 [0.883, 0.948] |
| new 46 | 0.953 [0.940, 0.970] |

Per snapshot 0.754–1.106; CI covers 1 for 44/56 and covers the rate-theory prediction for 52/56. ν/ν_Langer median 0.82 (0.34–1.15); γ/ω_b median 0.41. Theory ratio median 0.919, TST 0.948.

**Test against the paper.** Published RvAthreeSlopeRatio = 0.8905 [0.831, 0.950] on the 3 B3 snapshots. The new pooled 0.946 lies inside that interval: no contradiction. The new value is closer to 1 and about 5× narrower; reported as a finding. (A pooled fit restricted to the 3 B3 snapshots was started but not completed: not run.)
