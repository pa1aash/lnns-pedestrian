**Replays.** Complete for RVO2, drive, b*, SFM: 0 snapshots change outcome between replay and original at 600 s. Legacy and constraint replays incomplete (not run in full).

**Reading.** The 120 s cut used in the paper does not hide late releases: a pair still moving at 120 s is held, not slow.
