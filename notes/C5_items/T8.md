## Reading
Throttling the drive part's target does not lower its completion; if anything it rises by 8 runs (n.s.). The drive
part's advantage over force-coupled ORCA (48:15) does not come from an unthrottled target speed in dense crowds; the
failure classes of all three single-drive arms (drive, throttled drive, τ′) are the same at-rest keystone mix, while
force-coupled ORCA alone leaves runs moving at 600 s (its constraint part).
