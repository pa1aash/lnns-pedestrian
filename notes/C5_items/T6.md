## g. The τ′ SFM target speed and the density throttle

- Code (code/sim/steering/hybrid.py:324-336, `_desired_tau_prime`): τ′ = τ τ_o/(τ + τ_o), τ_o = params tau_orca
  = 0.5 s (:116); v_t = τ′ (W v0/τ + v0/τ_o); force m(v_t e − v)/τ′ passed to compute_desired_force with
  local_densities=None, so no second throttle. W (Weidmann, γ 1.913, ρ_max 5.4, ρ floored at 0.01, clipped to
