Runs: 60 at dt = 0.005 s and the same 60 at dt = 0.01 s (the latter reproduce data/revision/D3/d3_croma.jsonl and add
the overlap record). Process CPU 8.03 h, below the 10 CPU-h cap, so all 25 runs per variant were completed at both
time steps. The 100 dt = 0.01 s records (C3's 40 and these 60) reproduce D3 exactly (end time, completion, flow).

Estimators unchanged (D3_analyze.load_run, D_croma_integrity.d4, fix_a7). The flow-ratio interval is the delta method
on the log of the ratio of mean flows with **Welch–Satterthwaite** degrees of freedom (fix_a7.ratio_means, used by
D_croma_integrity.d4 for Sect. 7); it is not a Student interval. Fluctuation means use a Student t interval over runs.
Friction factor 2κh·dt/m at the largest agent overlap h (κ = 2.4e5 kg/(m s), m = 80 kg; explicit tangential-friction
update stable below 2).

The dt = 0.01 s rows equal the published 25-run D3 values (counts and ratios). Max overlaps are over all 25 runs and
all steps; none exceeds 5 cm in any step at either dt; the agent maxima fall at t = 0.15–1.17 s except the jamb-range

Checks:
- **Every dt = 0.005 s flow ratio lies inside the dt = 0.01 s interval** (and so inside the published one, which is the
  same interval).
- Paired by seed (25), geometric-mean flow(0.005)/flow(0.01) [t interval on the logs]: SFM 1.01 [0.98, 1.05]; jamb
  range 0.98 [0.96, 1.01]; ORCA 0.98 [0.96, 1.01]; noise 1.03 [0.86, 1.23]. Completion discordance (0.005 only : 0.01
  only) 1:2, 2:0, 4:3, 5:1; exact McNemar p = 1.0, 0.50, 1.0, 0.22.
- Fluctuation is unchanged for the noise-free variants (intervals overlap); under per-step noise it rises
  0.108 → 0.124 m/s with disjoint intervals, the k_BT_eff rise at fixed per-step σ seen in C3.
- With per-step noise at dt = 0.01 s, one run (seed 46) reaches a friction factor of 2.25 (overlap 3.75 cm at
  t = 1.17 s), above the explicit stability bound; at dt = 0.005 s the largest factor is 1.18 (overlap 3.92 cm, seed
  66). All other variants stay below 1.5 at dt = 0.01 s.
- Wall overlap: the largest wall overlap is larger at dt = 0.005 s (3.08–3.10 cm) than at 0.01 s (2.58–2.73 cm) in
  every variant. It is a single-step maximum over 25 runs; the agent overlaps, flows and counts do not show it.

Comparison with C3's 10-run result: the 25-run sets confirm it. The 10-run maxima (noise, dt 0.005 s: 3.33 cm
agent, 2.93 cm wall) are exceeded in the 15 added runs (3.92 / 3.10 cm), and the friction factor at 0.005 s rises from
1.00 to 1.18, still below 2.
